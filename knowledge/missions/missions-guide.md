Missions are specific tasks players can complete for rewards, such as credits and XP. They can come from either NPCs in the form of quests, or upon completing others in the missions menu.

## Delivery Missions

In “delivery” type missions, you need to take a Parcel to a specified location within a certain amount of time. A new delivery appears automatically every few minutes up to 20 minutes (2 minutes if you failed a delivery beforehand); Julian will text you with a notification in the bottom right corner if you have the Runner Phone. The pickup and dropoff locations are indicated with a bag and flag icon, respectively, both on your compass and as a marker on the screen. Parcels spawn in set spots and are not randomized; instead, they depend on your vicinity to them at the time you got the notification from Julian.

If you die, or fail to complete the delivery within the given time, you automatically fail and lose any potential credits you could've gotten (the amount is displayed in the fail message). Failing a delivery will cause the next one to come 4x faster than usual, and running around the map gives you delivery spawns faster.

For each successfully completed delivery, the customer pays in large sums of credits. Depending on how far away the dropoff location is, you'll get paid more or less credits for successfully delivering the Parcel to its destination. The district the Parcel has to be delivered to, or how fast you delivered it, does NOT matter, you will get the same amount of credits either way.
