## Overview

A Mongoose Studio is the development team behind Reborn and its predecessor Legacy. They're also responsible for creating other Roblox experiences, most of which are part of the PARKOUR franchise.

## Trivia

* A Mongoose Studio was founded by hudzell on April 14th, 2023.

* Technically, because A Mongoose Studio is just a rebrand of Parkour Dev, it was actually founded sometime during 2016.

* A Mongoose Studio was formerly known as Parkour Dev.

* A Mongoose Studio plans to start creating games outside of Roblox after Reborn is completed.

* A Mongoose Studio has made a total of 9 Roblox experiences so far.

* ParkourRework was the first publicly available version of Reborn, being an extremely experimental and W.I.P. version of it at the time. As of the game's proper development start, it has been permanently closed.
