## Overview

PARKOUR Reborn is an experience on Roblox inspired by the sport parkour, developed by A Mongoose Studio. It is a remake of PARKOUR Legacy, another Roblox experience created by A Mongoose Studio's owner (hudzell).

## Trivia

* PARKOUR Reborn is usually just called Reborn amongst the PARKOUR community.

* Reborn had a few delays to its release, changing a total of 2 times. With its original release date scheduled for December 25th, 2022 (Christmas)—which then got delayed to the Summer of 2023, and further postponed to November 3rd, 2023. Transitioning into the pre-alpha stage, instead of the originally promised beta stage.

* Reborn started development on February 8th, 2022.

* The general idea of Reborn was first made up back in 2019.

* Reborn has some lore, which will most likely be revealed slowly in the coming updates.

* Reborn is the second mainstream experience in the PARKOUR franchise.

* Reborn will NEVER have VR support. As stated by hudzell: it is nearly impossible to implement with how the game is designed, and a waste of development resources.

* Reborn's first ever sneak peek was posted sometime during February 2022. The exact date can no longer be traced, as the Discord server where it was shared, Legacy's, has been deleted due to it getting raided beyond repair.

* Reborn has referenced once in another Roblox experience, specifically in Silly Brawl.
