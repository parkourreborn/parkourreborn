## Usable Items

### Gear

* Quickbelt: Most likely a utility . It will allow you to bind consumables to buttons/keys for quick use. It will hold a certain amount of consumables, it currently doesn't have upgrades paths, and will instead have one set of upgrades which increases slots.

* If consumables are used without the Quickbelt, it will only work while stationary and grounded.

* Glaive: An Augment utilized for both combat and movement (its addition is not fully confirmed).

* One of the Glaive movement options would be a pole vault function.

* Paraglider: A Compound. Returning from Legacy, it'll function about the same where it lets you float in the air for longer.

* Arc Lantern: A Utility. Illuminates the surrounding area with green light, especially for Asylum. It will have more uses than just light.

* Air Bag: Unknown type. It will be a last resort fall damage reducer, like a portable pad.

#### Upgrades

* Glaive: Enforcer Glaive & Drifter Glaive.

* Boost Belt: Dash Belt & Boost Belt.

* NOTE: Boost Belt is not to be confused with Quickbelt. Boost Belt is a separate gear that was leaked/mentioned many years ago. It may be scrapped at this point, and there is no information on it.

#### Acquiring

Not all gear will be obtained in the same way. Some will be purchasable directly from a vendor, some will simply be found laying on the floor fully complete already just like resources, and some will need to be crafted with pieces scattered across many different places such as vendor's shops.

#### Mods

This is a tentative system that might replace the current predetermined levels and paths system for gear upgrading. This is historical at this point, as no update or information has been presented for a while.

Gear will have unique upgrade tiers/rarities that can be upgraded a certain number of times or ways to expand the amount of slots they can have for mods, with each mod having a specific size to it going up to 4; each slot can only take one mod, but the size of that slot determines the maximum size mod you can fit in it.

Most mods will do something different from the rest; a size 1 mod might be like a percentage increase to pull strength for the Grappler, while a size 4 mod might be the functionality of the 3rd level of the Swing Grappler, that being the ability to swing anywhere with the Grappler.

You can mix, match, and combine mods at any point to create your own personal playstyle and customized behaviors for your gears. The obtainment methods for mods will be: finding drops, buying directly, randomized crates (bought with tokens only), crafting (a few ones only, not all), and more.

##### Considered Mods

* Grappler

* Recharge ammo overtime while mid-air.

### Consumables

Short buffs in certain aspects such as movement. They can be used by going into your inventory menu, or by binding one to one of Quickbelt's slots. Various types of consumables will be sold by multiple vendors.

* Two unknown consumables will be featured in a future update.

## Cosmetics

Special items that you can customize your character with. Most will be bought solely with tokens, but some will accept credits too.

* ANIMATION PACKS: Replaceable variants of Movement's animations (entirely cosmetic, meaning the mechanics themselves won't be altered). They will come in sets, with each individual animation being swappable if preferred. [+]

* EMOTES: Emotes can be anything, ranging from dances and casual activities to various exercises. They're a way to socialize with others more, and you'll be able to do them anywhere at any time. [+]

* DAP UP EMOTE: An emote that initiates a dap between two players. [-]

* TRICKS: Cool tricks you can do while freerunning, such as flips mid-air. [+]

* PARTICLE EFFECTS: Particle effects will appear around you when impacting with terrain (such as by landing or Wall kicking), jumping, the Grappler's rope attaching to a surface, etc. [+]

## Musical

* SOUNDTRACK PAUSES: While you play the game, the soundtrack will occasionally play rather than constantly cycle through music tracks and will be synchronized across the server, meaning all players who are listening to the soundtrack are hearing the same thing simultaneously. [+]

* RADIO CHANNELS: Radio channels will play music constantly and can be tuned into whenever you want. [+]

* DISTRICT THEMED: Special songs for each District. [-]

* TOWER OST'S: Massive towers which are only accessible through their interior (such as Vertigo) will eventually have their own soundtracks when scaling them. [+]

* UNLOCKABLE PLAYLISTS: Music made specifically by the community unlockable via an NPC. [-]

## Environmental

* TRAIN/MONORAIL: Will roam around the city, acting as a means of transportation by jumping on top of it to hitch a ride. It will most likely come out after full release, since it requires the entire map to be finished first. [-]

* BILLBOARD ADVERTISING: Not to be confused with hackable billboards. Players might be able to pay for advertising space on certain billboards (such as the ones in the times square area of Fragment) using decals. [-]

* EXTRA DISTRICTS/CITIES: Rather than being part of Verna, future locations may be their own DLC Maps (e.g. Neon). [-]

* HIGHWAYS AND PARKS: Hangout places in the city, making Verna more realistic. [-]

* DISTRICT RE-DOs: Some districts like The Mirror are planned to be re-done in some capacity (Stack and Fragment are already reworked, unknown if Skyarc will be reworked). [+]

* VERTEX REWORK: Because of its age and subsequently short height for the modern times, it'll receive a complete rework. [+]

* VERTICAL ZIPLINES: A special behavior for ziplines positioned vertically. [-]

* WINDOW LIGHT TEXTURES: Dirwik and other districts/areas having window light textures when it's night in the same way as Downtown. [+]

* OBSCURED SPOTS: More hard-to-reach places similar to Clover Drift. [+]

* SWING POINTS ON BUILDINGS: May be changed from their red cubes to something else. [-]

* MORE TYPES OF NPCs: More types of NPC's will be eventually added, such as civilians or CPD guards. [+]

* STANDING ON DRONES: The ability to stand on drones. [-]
