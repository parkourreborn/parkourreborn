The PARKOUR Reborn Wiki is a hub for all information regarding the game. Our goal is to make a high quality resource for people to access information about the game. This website is intended to provide consistent documentation on everything about the game and its history. All content is written and maintained by volunteers, and is supported by official A Mongoose Studio staff.

 HISTORY

| 18 December 2022 | The wiki was born, initially created on Fandom and founded by Lightning789 (bureaucrat/administrator). | The wiki was created much before the game was first released in order to prepare for what was to come. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 November 2023 | PARKOUR Reborn pre-alpha is released and accessible by payment. | Wiki content begins to pick up, with basic information locations, interface, time trials, and update history being added. | Around this time, other users are discovering that the wiki exists, and a few people begin to contribute to it. |
| 11 November 2023 | Movement Techs become more prominent, and the movement page hosts a section for them. | Around this time, people are coming up with different names for these techs, and adding their own to the page. | Movement Techs were eventually locked to administration to officiate the process more smoothly. |
| 23 November 2023 | PARKOUR Reborn alpha is released and accessible by payment. | Wiki becomes official! |
| 24 May 2025 | The move off of Fandom begins, and recreating the new wiki on Miraheze is started. |
| 4 August 2025 | PARKOUR Reborn officially moves off of fandom and on to Miraheze. |
