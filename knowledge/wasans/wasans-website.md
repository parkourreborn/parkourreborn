The Wasans website is wasans.tully.sh. Logging in works with Discord, Google or Roblox.

## Main pages

* **Leaderboard**: every Parkour Reborn time-trial player, ranked by Wasans score (the average of their best score on every trial).
* **Trials**: world records and full leaderboards for every trial. Opening a trial shows every runner, the platinum and bronze times Wasans scores with, and how the record has fallen over time.
* **Combos**: the combo leaderboards for each category.
* **Submissions**: every trial run and combo submitted, newest first, with its status (pending, approved or denied) and video.
* **Submit**: send in new PBs or combos with their video.

## More pages

* **Calculator**: type over any time to see what it would do to a score ("what 2% off a PB is worth, or platinum on a trial you haven't run"), or add a second player to line them up trial by trial. The faster time on each trial shows green and the slower red.
* **Analytics**: every trial's spread of times, activity over time (runs submitted and approved, PBs, world records, new and active players), the biggest improvers and climbers, who holds the most world records, and a deep dive per trial (record progression, monthly run times, volume and top grinders).
* **Prizes**: active prizes and giveaways.
* **WR archive**: the original file of every world record ever set, exactly as it was submitted, kept for good and free to download.
* **Rules**: what makes a run valid.
* **Scoring & FAQ**: how scores and tiers work, member applications and common questions.

## Player profiles

A profile shows a player's score, tier, rank, world records, PBs on every trial with their rank on it, combos, recent runs, a score-over-time chart (marking PBs, new world records, and other players' records that lowered their score), a rank-over-time chart and their peak rank. Players also see their own "biggest gains": the trials where improving would raise their score the most.

## The world record archive and the old WR Drive

Before the website, world records were kept as videos in a shared Google Drive folder ("the old WR Drive"). Staff brought those videos into the site, so old records show up in each trial's record history and in the archive. Archive files that came from the old WR Drive are marked as such and don't count toward scores. A record whose player never signed up is shown under a placeholder player with just a name, left out of leaderboards and search; if that player later signs up, staff move the runs to their account.

## Other features

* Site-wide announcements from the owners appear as a dismissible banner.
* A video compiling every world record is rendered automatically every month.
* Search covers players, player ids, times and trials.
* Parkour Reborn Hub's own developer, ElkkuT, has contributed to the Wasans website.
