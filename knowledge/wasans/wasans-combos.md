## Combo leaderboards

Besides time trials, Wasans keeps combo leaderboards: the highest combo score a player has reached, ranked per category. The categories are named after the gear setup the combo is done with:

* **Mag** (Mag Rail)
* **Gearless** (no gear)
* **Yank** (Yank Grappler)
* **Swing** (Swing Grappler)

Staff can add, rename or reorder categories, so the list can change.

Combos are a fully separate leaderboard. They never change your Wasans score, PBs, world records or time-trial ranks. Each player has one entry per category: their best approved combo.

## Submitting a combo

Send in a combo with its YouTube video on the Submit page. A moderator checks it before it counts. The combo rules:

* The clip must show the full combo, from 0 all the way to the count you are submitting.
* Your username must be visible, and the final combo count must show in chat (or on your profile if chat doesn't post it).
* Combos under 100k score are not accepted.
* Clips longer than 25 minutes are not allowed.
* Combos are submitted with a YouTube link, and the count as a whole number.

Combo submissions can be reviewed by combo moderators as well as the general moderators.
