Rules are numbered on the Wasans Rules page so moderators can point at one ("denied per 3.2").

The short version: show your final time after you finish, and have proper video quality.

## 1. Run rules

* 1.1 Any glitches are prohibited. This exists as a wildcard; the ones currently banned are listed under Banned glitches.
* 1.2 Using autoparkour or autotransition is prohibited and automatically invalidates the run.
* 1.3 No autoclickers or macros.
* 1.4 Practice mode is not allowed.

## 2. Banned glitches

* 2.1 Any form of teleportation glitch.
* 2.2 Double vaulting. It is only doable with autoparkour, so it is banned regardless.
* 2.3 Coil slide on a slope. It is extremely buggy and can give a random speed boost depending on your FPS. A run is still invalid if a slope coil slide happens on flat ground or a non-steep slope because of a glitch.
* 2.4 Moves that are not technically possible, such as the "wasans boost".
* 2.5 Afterboost overriding your current velocity, also known as "drophop".

Anything not on this list is still covered by rule 1.1.

## 3. Video requirements

* 3.1 The clip must include the entire run, along with the actual time after exiting the trial.
* 3.2 The clip must clearly show a properly visible timer, your username, and the game build number at the bottom of the screen.
* 3.3 No overlays may cover the footage: images, PNGs, videos, stream layouts, text or anything else drawn over the video. Handcams and keyboard overlays are fine, up to moderator discretion.
* 3.4 Video quality is on you. If the needed details are not clearly visible, the run may be rejected at the score moderator's discretion.
* 3.5 Cut the clip tight enough that the start, run and finish are convincing, without cutting any of the required details.

## 4. Submitting runs

* 4.1 Upload the video file, or paste a Medal clip link.
* 4.2 Enter your time with no more than three decimal places.
* 4.3 Your time has to at least reach the trial's gold medal time.
* 4.4 You cannot submit a time slower than your current PB, so your record stays clean.
* 4.5 You can send several runs at once from the Submit page: drop your videos anywhere on it and each one becomes a run.
* 4.6 World-record runs must be submitted within an acceptable amount of time after they were recorded. Normal life delays are fine, like being on vacation or going to bed right after the run. "Acceptable" means acceptable to other members: wanting to gatekeep it, or forgetting to upload it for a week, does not count.

## 5. Accounts

* 5.1 If you are permanently banned from the game, you cannot submit runs, to follow the main game rule.
* 5.2 Alt accounts are allowed only if you are not ban evading.
* 5.3 Staff can ban a player from submitting. A banned player keeps their account, approved runs and score, but cannot submit anything new until the ban is lifted.

## 6. Combo rules

* 6.1 The clip must show the full combo, from 0 all the way to the count you are submitting.
* 6.2 Your username must be visible, and the final combo count must show in chat (or on your profile if chat does not post it).
* 6.3 Combos under 100k score are not accepted.
* 6.4 Clips longer than 25 minutes are not allowed.
* 6.5 Combos are submitted with a YouTube link.

## 7. Moderation

* 7.1 Every submission starts as pending. Moderators can move it between pending, approved and denied at any time.
* 7.2 A run that is technically within the rules but exploits a loophole can still be rejected.
* 7.3 Moderators can reject a score for any reason, without explanation.

## Rules FAQ

* **What counts as a glitch?** "Any glitches are prohibited" only exists as a wildcard. The ones actually banned right now are the banned glitches above.
* **Can I autoclick or macro?** No.
* **My video quality is low. Is this fine?** If the needed details are not clearly visible, the run may be rejected. If you keep your bitrate low, that is on you.
* **I didn't beat my PB. How do I show my actual time?** The actual time is not shown after exiting the trial when you don't beat your PB. Open the developer console (F9) and search for "hudzell's great pain reduced your time by (time)". Subtract that from your timer time, then round to three decimals. The pain can even be negative: 6.974 − 0.0004292 = 6.9735708 → 6.974, and 6.974 − (−0.0004292) = 6.9744292 → 6.974. Just have both the timer time and the hudzell pain clearly visible in the clip; the score moderator confirms the time, and the Submit page's "Time not shown?" option does the subtraction for you.
