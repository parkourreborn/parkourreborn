## Who Wasans is

Wasans is a Korean player from the older Parkour Legacy era. The Wasans server name and its role names grew out of community lore around that player and that time.

## The Wasans message

The lore starts with a message Wasans posted in broken English, defending themselves in the old ranked scene. In it, Wasans:

* admits to having been boosted once, but says they reached Master through their own routes;
* argues that making a route is a skill, because it takes effort and skill to see a gap and then do it;
* asks the people who gave them trouble in ranked "Why do you call me router when you can't even win?";
* says not every Korean player was boosted, and that "chr" and "headrog" were not rank trading but lost data while ranked;
* says permanently banned players used alt accounts because they still wanted to play the game.

The message is kept word for word in the Wasans website's code repository and is the source of the community's in-jokes.

## Router

"Router" is what Wasans was called by others, and it is now the highest Wasans tier: a Wasans score of 0.900 or more. A router is someone who finds and masters their own routes.

## The Lore Book of Wasans

The community also wrote "The Lore Book of Wasans", a tongue-in-cheek story built on the message. Its first chapter, "The Rise of the Wasans", has three parts:

* **The Beginning**: the first Wasan, a runner who was neither the fastest nor the strongest, says creating routes is a skill and an art. Runners who felt misjudged gather around the idea that creativity and resilience matter as much as raw speed.
* **The Fall of the Old Ranks**: the old ranking system, broken by data loss, rank-trading accusations and unfair bans, is rejected. "chr" and "headrog" become symbols of players wrongly accused who kept their skill.
* **The Alt Accords**: banned runners come back on alternate accounts, not to cheat but because they love the game too much to stay away.

It is community humour, not official game lore.
