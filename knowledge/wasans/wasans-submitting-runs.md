## How to submit a run

1. **Record it** with the timer, your username and the game build number in view. Include the whole run and the time shown after you exit the trial.
2. **Log in** on the Wasans website (wasans.tully.sh) with Discord, Google or Roblox. One account can have a Discord login, a Google login and any number of linked Roblox accounts. Linking a Roblox account can be made a requirement for submitting.
3. **Submit the clip** on the Submit page: upload the video file or paste a Medal clip link, pick the trial and enter your time (up to three decimals). You can drop several videos at once and each becomes its own run.
4. **Wait for a moderator.** Every run starts as pending. A moderator checks the video and approves or denies it. Only approved runs count toward your score.

Your time must at least reach the trial's gold medal time, and it cannot be slower than your current PB. If the game didn't show your final time because it wasn't a PB, use the "Time not shown?" option, which works it out from the hudzell pain line in the developer console.

## What happens to your run

* The video is processed into an MP4 (up to 1080p and 60 fps) with a thumbnail. Uploads can be up to 500 MB and 10 minutes long; any common video format works.
* If you have a Discord account linked, the run is posted to the Wasans Discord automatically, with your name, your Discord ID, the trial and the time.
* Your profile, scores, submissions, personal bests, world records, the moderator notes on your runs and your proof videos are public, on the site and through its API. The proof video is reachable by its link from the moment it is uploaded, even while pending or if denied.
* Each new run is stamped with the current game version.
* The untouched original file is kept privately for moderators and deleted 7 days after the run is approved or denied. Runs that were ever a world record keep their original for good in the public WR archive.

## Getting on the leaderboards

Every approved run is a PB on that trial's leaderboard. Your Wasans score, your overall rank and your tier role update from your approved PBs. A trial you have no approved run on counts as 0 in your score, so submitting a time on every trial raises it.
