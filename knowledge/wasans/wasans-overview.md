## What Wasans is

Wasans is the competitive time-trial community of Parkour Reborn. It is not only a website: it is a Discord server and a group of runners who submit their time-trial runs, get them checked by moderators, and are ranked against each other with the Wasans score.

The community is named after Wasans, a Korean player from the older Parkour Legacy era. The server name and its role names grew out of community lore around that history (see Wasans Origin and Lore).

Wasans is a community project, not a company. It is run by the website operators and project maintainers of the Wasans score and submission community.

## The parts of Wasans

* **The Discord server**: where the community talks, where every submitted run is posted for review, and where tier roles are handed out automatically from each player's score. Invite: discord.gg/9pnRYDU6wg (the server's rules post always has the active invite).
* **The Wasans website** (wasans.tully.sh): world records, leaderboards for every trial, the overall leaderboard, combo leaderboards, run submissions, a score calculator, analytics, prizes and giveaways, the world record archive, the rules, and the Scoring & FAQ page.
* **The Wasans score**: a single number from 0 to 1 that says how good a player's time-trial times are compared to the current world records. It is the number people show in their Discord names.
* **The Wasans bot**: a Discord bot that posts submitted runs, keeps score roles and nicknames up to date, and answers slash commands like /leaderboard and /wrs.

## How to take part

1. Join the Discord server.
2. Record your time-trial runs with the timer, your username and the game build number visible.
3. Log in on the Wasans website and submit each run (upload the video or paste a Medal clip link).
4. A moderator approves or denies it. Approved runs count toward your Wasans score, your rank on the leaderboards and your tier role.
5. With a score of at least 0.300 you can apply to become an official member of the server.

Wasans only covers time trials and combos. It does not track in-game levels, credits or other progression.
