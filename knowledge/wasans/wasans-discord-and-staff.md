## The Discord server

The Wasans Discord is where the time-trial community talks and where runs are reviewed. Use the invite shared by staff; the server's rules post always has the active one: discord.gg/9pnRYDU6wg.

* Every run submitted on the website by someone with a linked Discord account is posted to the server automatically.
* Tier roles (from @unranked up to @router) are given out automatically from each player's Wasans score, and nicknames show the score.
* Site announcements and live giveaway posts also appear there.

## Becoming a member

You need a Wasans score of at least 0.300 (the Platinum tier, about platinum on every trial on average) to become an official member of the server.

1. Estimate your score on the Calculator, which uses the current world records.
2. Have proof ready for every run you are counting on.
3. Submit each run on the Submit page so moderators can approve them directly.
4. Once your runs are approved, staff use the resulting score to decide your role or member application.

## Staff

* **Combo moderators** review combo submissions.
* **Junior moderators** review trial runs and combos, but cannot delete submissions.
* **Senior moderators** review everything and can delete submissions.
* **Owners** run the site: trials, game versions, announcements, prizes, giveaways and submission bans.

Moderators can move any submission between pending, approved and denied, and can reject a run for any reason. Staff can also ban a player from submitting without touching their account or approved runs.

## The Wasans bot

The bot answers four slash commands, each with a card in the site's style:

* **/leaderboard [board] [player]**: the overall leaderboard by default, or any trial, any combo category, or the top 3 of every combo category. With a player, it opens the page that player is on with their row highlighted.
* **/submissions [player] [on] [status] [run]**: runs, newest first, filtered by player, trial or combo category, and status; or one run's card.
* **/stats [player]**: a profile card with score, tier and progress, rank, world records, PBs, runs, best combo and latest runs. Defaults to whoever ran it.
* **/wrs [trial]**: every trial's world record, or with a trial, that trial's world record history: each record, its improvement and how long it stood.

Every player option takes a Wasans name, an @mention, or "me". Mentions are matched through the player's linked Discord login, never by guessing a name.
