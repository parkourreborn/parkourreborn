The Prizes page on the Wasans website lists the active prizes and giveaways: "Win prizes by setting records and ranking up, and join giveaways for a chance at more."

## Prizes

A prize is tied to a goal. Hit the goal and it's yours; staff confirm each winner before it's official. A prize's goal is one of:

* a new world record on a trial (a specific trial or any);
* a new combo world record (a specific category or any);
* ranking up to a higher tier;
* reaching a target Wasans score.

The site spots qualifying events automatically and puts them in front of staff to confirm or reject. A prize can have a limited number of winners and an end date.

## Giveaways

* A giveaway is a raffle with a set number of winners and a deadline.
* Any logged-in player can join, once per giveaway, before the deadline.
* Winners are drawn at random when it ends; staff can reroll if needed.
* The Wasans bot keeps a live giveaway post up to date in the Discord server.

When nothing is running, the page says "No prizes up for grabs right now" or "No giveaways running right now. Check back soon."
