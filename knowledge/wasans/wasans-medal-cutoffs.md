Wasans scores every trial against two cutoffs: a platinum time (scores 0.300) and a bronze time (scores 0.000). The world record scores 1.000. These are the cutoffs in the Wasans website's scoring code, in seconds:

| Trial | Platinum | Bronze |
| :---- | ----: | ----: |
| Crystal | 10.5 | 25 |
| Genesis | 12.5 | 70 |
| Glass | 12.5 | 30 |
| Riser | 10.75 | 25 |
| Solar | 18 | 40 |
| Vestibule | 10.5 | 24 |
| Celsius | 12 | 25 |
| Circulation | 14 | 35 |
| Flow | 16.5 | 32 |
| Martyr | 12.5 | 35 |
| Neon Bold | 25 | 40 |
| Sawdust | 16.5 | 40 |
| Ascension | 12 | 25 |
| Faith | 15 | 30 |
| Gale | 8.25 | 15 |
| Grip | 17 | 35 |
| Thread | 14 | 25 |
| Needle | 13 | 30 |
| Umbrel | 23 | 35 |
| Depot | 19.5 | 50 |
| Flame | 13.5 | 30 |
| Ironsing | 17.5 | 29 |
| Monoxide | 14.5 | 27 |
| Rust Belt | 18.5 | 35 |
| Wisp | 13 | 22 |

Whether a trial currently counts toward the score average depends on its status: newly added, removed and changed trials go through a 7-day grace period (see Wasans Score and Tiers).
