## Overview

Marcel is an NPC introduced in alpha 1.73. He is located in a bridge section of Zephyr and can be reached by climbing the exterior of the tower instead of progressing through the interior as normal. He currently does nothing, but will presumably have some use in the future. Like Harper and Derek, he has two different spawn locations in the same vicinity.

## Trivia

* Marcel is the second NPC to be added in to fragment after Harper.

* He is arguably one of the hardest NPCs to reach, as he is located very high up and requires climbing the exterior of Zephyr, one of the tallest towers in the game.

* He holds the same idle pose as Derek, Mantra, and Petra, while having a similar talking animation as Julian.
