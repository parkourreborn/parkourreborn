## Info
* Type: Vendor
* Minimum Rank: None
* Sells: Resources, Fittings, Duct Tape, Rugged Synthetics, V.D.W. Tape, Kinetic Fabric, Gear / Key Items, Runner Phone, Zipline Hook
* Location(s): Downtown, On the building with the Solar Time Trial, in one of 5 spots
* Gender: Male

## Overview

Derek is the second NPC that players will likely encounter after Julian. He periodically moves to different locations in the vicinity of the Solar time trial. He's the vendor who will sell you the Runner Phone, which is necessary for progressing further into the story. Players must complete Julian’s initiation questline before they can speak to him. Additionally, he will give you Binoculars if you complete his questline at rank 5.

## Workshop

Derek's Workshop is located on the same building Derek stands on, accessible via a gray door across from the Solar time trial. Inside is a messy work station with different props, many of which include resources found around the map for crafting other gear. There are also some posters, a laptop, tarps, and a note that reads
"TO DO LIST:

- PICK UP MORE SYNTHETICS

- ADJUST GRAPPLER ROPE

- TEST DURABILITY OF NEW PROTOTYPE"
