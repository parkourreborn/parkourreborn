* Minimum Rank: 8
* Sells: Bounding Fluctuator, Aluminum Casing, Pulse Generator, Rail Trigger, Gauss Amplifier, Magnetic Coil
* Location(s): Fragment, At the Ascension time trial building; Aven
* Gender: Female

## Overview

Harper is an NPC located at Aven in Fragment. Just like Petra, she requires the rank card obtained from Julian in order to interact with her further. She also has a minimum requirement of rank 8 in order to access her shop. She is a vendor who sells you the parts necessary for creating the Mag Rail. If you're rank 20 or higher, she will have different dialogue. Just like Derek, she will periodically move to different spots on the same building.

## Trivia

* Harper arrived in alpha 1.5.

* She is the first NPC to be introduced in Fragment.

* Along with the other NPCs, Harper is presumably associated with Julian, as she references him in her dialogue for players without a rank card.

* Prior to alpha 1.51, you needed to be rank 10 to access her shop.

* She is one of three NPCs that have different spawn locations, with the other two being Derek and Marcel. Like Marcel, she only has two spawns, which is less compared to Derek's 5.
