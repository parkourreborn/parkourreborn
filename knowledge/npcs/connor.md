## Overview

Connor is an NPC located in the Workshop, a small interior in Dirwik accessible via an elevator next to the Celsius time trial. He is a vendor who sells the parts needed to craft the Springhook. Like other vendors, Connor requires a rank card to interact with him further. He has a minimum rank requirement of 6.

## Workshop

Connor is located directly in his Workshop, a small underground area accessible via an elevator or a door closer to the ground. The elevator can be found right behind the Celsius time trial in Dirwik, with Connor's decal clearly marked above the garage door. Taking the elevator will bring you to a single floor space decorated with various workshop equipment, boxes, folding chairs, tables, and blue tarps hanging from the ceiling. The resources necessary to make the Springhook can be seen displayed towards the back of the room.

## Trivia

* Connor arrived in alpha 1.52.

* His workshop was added earlier in alpha 1.5. The area was also more barren.

* He was the first and NPC to have his own dedicated area where he works.

* Derek has one as well, located on the same building where he spawns.

* He is the only NPC that guides you on how to collect the necessary parts for a gear, as well as having the locations for the resources marked by his own decal.

* He only sells the resources for Springhook.

* Connor has the longest dialogue in the game, often going on tangents when first interacted with by the player.

* Connor's avatar was designed by Discord user tisumi_.

* He is the only NPC who has 3 dialogue options available at once during one of his interactions.

* Connor's Workshop originally housed the Pulse Generator before being moved to Dirwik Construction's Resource Branch.
