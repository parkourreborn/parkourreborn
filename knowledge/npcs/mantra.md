## Overview

Mantra is an NPC initially introduced during alpha 1.3, located in the southwest part of Downtown at her hideout. She currently doesn't do anything other than briefly conversing with the player, but will help guide and explain gear in the future.

## Hideout

Mantra's Hideout is located at the identically named Mantra Hideout checkpoint, located south of the Dropper challenge. It is a rock-climbing venue with recreational activities such as table tennis and pool, along with lounging areas and seating. Mantra is positioned somewhere midway between the floors, and presumably oversees the area.

## Trivia

* The interior where Mantra is located used to house one of the two accidentally left in Rugged Synthetic resources back in alpha 1.08.

* In alpha 1.73, she received an actual avatar instead of the default placeholder one (the same one Mike has).

* Her hideout area also received a reworked interior, featuring various rock climbing decorations, recreational game decorations, and indoor seating. It was much more barren and empty prior to this update.
