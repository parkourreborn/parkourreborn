## Info
* Type: Vendor
* Minimum Rank: 3
* Sells: Override Module, Winch Mount, Nanotube Cable, Magnetized Alloy Hook, Micro Pulse Driver, Fittings, Cable Glider
* Location(s): Dirwik, Directly across the crane, near PRK Tech.
* Gender: Female

## Overview

Petra is the second vendor that players will likely encounter. By interacting with her, she reveals that she's associated with Julian, an NPC in Downtown. She is a vendor who sells many of the resources necessary to craft the Grappler and its upgrades. She also sells the Cable Glider gear. The player must be at least rank 3 or higher to access her shop; if you're rank 10 or higher, she will have different dialogue.
