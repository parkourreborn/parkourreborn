## Overview

Currency is used to purchase items, whether it be resources from NPC vendors, or gear/passes from the Store. There are 2 types of currency: standard ( credits), and premium ( tokens). Credits are obtained through various in-game methods, while tokens are bought with Robux, being gifted by other players, or by reaching the 7th day in Daily Rewards.

All items are purchasable with either currency, but gear generally cost much more in tokens. Anything bought with either that was affected by a data reset will be fully refunded automatically.

## Credits

Credits are the standard currency for buying things in the game. The Executive Runner gamepass doubles the amount of credits earned.

### Valuables

Valuables come in the form of functionless items spread across the map that can be sold at vendors for credits. They are client-sided, meaning other players can't take them from you.

#### Piles

Piles are where Common tier valuables spawn on top of, respawning every few minutes once looted; if you're situated near them, respawning will be disabled until you leave their radius, and will resume counting down once done. The three types are: Concrete Bricks, Paper Sheets, and Steel Beams. They can easily be spotted by their red accent coloring.

#### Tiers

#### Scanner Module

A premium upgrade bought from the Store that allows you to see valuables from afar and through walls, shown by 2 tier colored/sized translucent semi-circles appearing around each one.

### Deliveries

Main article: Missions

After you acquire the Runner Phone, Julian will call you every few minutes up to 20 minutes about a delivery near you. Once you pickup the requested Parcel, a timer starts to count down. If you don't deliver the Parcel in time, or die doing so, you fail the delivery. Successfully completing a delivery rewards credits based on the distance to the dropoff point.

## Tokens

Tokens are the premium alternative to buying items with credits at times, and are exclusively used to buy premium items. They can be purchased from the tab menu for Robux by clicking the wallet icon directly next to your credits and tokens count.

## Daily Rewards

Players can redeem rewards in the form of currency every 24 hours, and the interface can be found in the top left corner of the screen. Credits are earned for the first 6 days, and some tokens are earned on the 7th. Currency rewards double after the first week passes, while tokens increase by 5. Executive Runner doubles the credits received from daily rewards.

## Trivia

* In alpha 1.7, the credits was capped to 5,000,000 one time in order to acknowledge previous exploitative methods to get credits. This is not a permanent cap, and players can still gain over this amount.

* Executive Runner used to double your daily token reward amount, which was an unintended bug. It was fixed in update alpha 1.7.

* The Smartphone used to be an Uncommon tier, but was eventually changed to Rare.

* Prior to alpha 1.7, the sell amount multipliers that came from certain NPCs was different, specifically for trash and scrap:

* For trash and scrap, both Derek and Harper offered to pay one third of their value rounded up.

* For scrap only, Petra offered to pay one half of its value rounded up.

* The remaining 6 valuable types did not exhibit a difference in sell value between all 4 vendors.

* This made Connor the most profitable NPC to sell to at the time, as he bought every valuable from you at full price.

* As of alpha 1.73, valuables are populated when the player joins, so there is no initial wait for them to spawn.
