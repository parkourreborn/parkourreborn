The store offers a variety of upgrades and benefits purchasable with either tokens or Robux, both in-game and on the game's page store tab. It can be accessed within the Tab Menu.
 For items sold by vendors, see Marketplace.

## Premium Items

In-game items purchased with tokens (prices below are in tokens) that provide benefits, both permanent and temporary.

* **Signal Booster** (Permanent Upgrade, 175): Respawn anywhere, infinitely

* **Scanner Module** (Permanent Upgrade, 300): Detect collectible items in your vicinity, even through walls!

* **Warp Core** (Permanent Upgrade, 300): Instantly teleport between any previously unlocked checkpoints or races

* **Paint Palette** (Permanent Upgrade, 100): Choose between 12 different UI themes
