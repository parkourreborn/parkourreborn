## Overview

Multipliers are a mechanic that increase the amount of experience points (XP for short) gained from performing movements or completing activities. They often allow you to level up faster than you normally would, and come in two forms: combo and XP Routers.

## Combo

The combo box

Combo is unlocked at level 10, and allows the user to gain significant amounts of XP by acquiring a high score.

### How It Works

#### Combo Loss

Stopping for a bit, taking serious damage, or dying (note that dying still rewards XP) will end a combo. Combos do not end while clutching onto something, allowing you to use them as "rest spots."

#### Combo Circles

Whenever you start to move around, an invisible circle is formed around you. Once you touch the ground after exiting all previously laid circles, your combo will increase, and a new, larger circle will be laid at your current location; this is indicated by the '+COVERAGE' pop-up next to the combo box, alongside a soft ping noise.

#### Colored Numbers

* Big & Red (Bottom Left): Your current multiplier. Capping at x10000, it increases with the total map area covered by all previously laid circles.

* Small & Gray (Top Right): Your RAW score. It increases by a certain amount depending on how fast you leave your circles.

* Medium & White (Top Left): Your total score. It is your RAW score multiplied by your current multiplier. This number alone affects how much XP you get at the end of a combo.

## XP Routers

An XP Router

XP Routers permanently increase your XP multiplier, granting a bonus XP percentage upon activating; the percentage varies by location; easily accessible routers grant +5%, while those on tall towers grant +50%. The maximum achievable multiplier from activating every single one of them is x12.40. Their appearance resembles that of a small standing satellite dish next to a laptop.

XP Routers don't affect every XP gaining method:

### AFFECTED

* XP Gain

* Combo

### UNAFFECTED

* Rewards

* Races

* Missions
