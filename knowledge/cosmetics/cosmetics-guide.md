Cosmetics are decorative features, with the current ones known as skins. They can be applied to a variety of gear, and can be bought from the in-game Store via bags. In the future, there will be other cosmetics, such as animations and emotes.

## SKINS & BAGS

Skins are a form of cosmetic that changes the appearance of individual gears. They do not affect gameplay, and each skin is specific to a single gear type (Grappler, Mag Rail, Glove, Springhook, Zipline Hook, or Cable Glider). In other words, they're obtained separately for each specific gear.

They can be obtained by opening bags and getting a random one. Each bag has a unique theme, with the current ones being the Starter, Runner, Gallery, Worker, and Shade bag. Bags cost credits or tokens, with credits being much more expensive than tokens. By default, bags grant a random skin for a random gear, but players can target a specific gear using Prefer Slips.

Another way to obtain skins is through the Night Market, a shop that rotates over time with various skins to buy directly. Both bags and the Night Market are accessible from the in-game Store within the Tab Menu. Holiday skins only appear during their normal seasonal period. Night Market cards are colored by rarity.

Different skins have different rarities: common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, ultimate, and mythical. The rates for different skins vary, with a common skin having a higher chance of being rolled compared to a legendary. Additionally, you can not get duplicates of the same skin.

### Prefer Slips

Prefer Slips can be used to assign a bag to a specific gear, so the cosmetic received will only be for that gear instead of a random one. Each one costs 25 tokens and can be purchased from the store under the bags tab. They are consumed when opening cosmetics.

## Rates

Pity guarantees a skin of a specified rarity after enough bags are opened without receiving one. It doesn't replace a higher rarity result with a lower rarity reward. Pity progress can be viewed from the bag interface.

## Trivia

* Skins were first introduced in alpha 1.7, and are the first type of cosmetics to be added to the game.

* Some skins are related to or from PARKOUR Legacy.

* The Legacy skin represents the way gears looked in Legacy, including the Grappler, Glove, and Mag Rail.

* The method of obtaining skins is very similar to Legacy, where players open bags and receive a random skin. The difference is Reborn's bags are obtained via currency ( tokens and credits), whereas Legacy bags were collectable from around the map.

* Reborn skins are specific to each gear (e.g. Grappler's Salmon skin and Glove's Salmon skin are obtained separately), whereas Legacy's skins could be applied to any compatible gear.

* Basic Blue is the only "basic" color that is specific to the glove, since the other gears use it by default.

* The Worker and Shade bags were introduced in alpha 1.73.

* alpha 1.73 introduced the ultimate and mythical rarities.
