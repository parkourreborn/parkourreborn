Crafting is the primary mechanic in Reborn that allows players to obtain gear. Resources are either dispersed around the map at fixed spawn locations, or sold by NPC vendors for credits and tokens. Collecting from both of these sources are necessary to craft higher and higher tier gear.

NOTE: Not all gears are obtained via crafting. For more information on a gear, view its dedicated page.

## Overview

Crafting is a game mechanic that allows player to combine various materials, collectively called Resources, to make certain gear. By placing the resources in the correct slots of the crafting grid, the CRAFT button becomes clickable, allowing players to create a new gear or upgrade existing ones.

Resources are obtained throughout the map and from NPC vendors. In order to craft all available variants of a gear, players will have to obtain resources from both of these sources. Most vendors sell specific resources for specific gear: Derek sells many beginner level items, Petra sells mostly Grappler parts, Harper sells Mag Rail parts, and Connor sells Springhook parts.

Alternatively, gear can be bought directly from the Store with tokens. The currently released gears, upgrades, and their respective resource locations can be found below.

## Trivia

* Crafting was first added back in alpha 1.0 alongside the release of the Grappler.

* This makes the Grappler the first ever gear to utilize crafting as its way of obtainment.

* Many resources didn't have a proper model when they were added initially, so they had a PLACEHOLDER cube.

* Most were given their proper model in alpha 1.5. Some existing models were changed to a different one, such as the Override Module and the Winch Mount.

* The remaining missing models were added in alpha 1.52.

* The Winch Mount resource used to be called "Grappler Housing" before the release of alpha 1.4.

* One of the rugged synthetics in Fragment was accidentally removed in alpha 1.72, but was promptly re-added.
