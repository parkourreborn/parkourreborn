## Overview

Spawn regions, often referred to as just transceivers, are a mechanic that allows players to respawn from the last place they were standing on the ground (akin to Legacy's respawning system).

To activate one, approach the laptop beneath the transceiver and hold down CONTEXT ACTION when the prompt appears. The laptop's screen will go from red to blue, and the notification 'SPAWN REGION UNLOCKED' will appear in the bottom right of your screen.

As the name implies, spawn regions do not cover the entirety of Verna; instead, they depend on your proximity to an activated transceiver. If the player moves too far away from the transceiver, their signal to it is lost, causing respawning to revert to the checkpoint system. There can be either 3 or more/less transceivers in each district.

### Respawn Lives

When respawning using a transceiver, your signal to it will dwindle, shown by the HUD icon depleting slowly (similarly to a Wi-Fi signal). Once you run out of respawn lives, respawning reverts to using the checkpoint system. Syncing to a checkpoint restores all of your respawn lives. Each new checkpoint discovered adds 1 max respawn life, up to a total of 10 in each district. The exact number of remaining respawn lives are shown right above the HUD icon whenever you die, then fade out slowly.

## Trivia

* The term spawn regions and respawn transceivers are often used interchangeably.

* The HUD icon blinking is an indication that it has updated to record your last valid standing position, meaning you will respawn there when you die.

* The signal booster premium upgrade can be purchased for 175 to respawn anywhere infinitely without the need of a transceiver.

* The location of Vertigo's transceiver changed a few times while the tower was being revamped.

* Prior to alpha 1.73, global chat was only available within range of an activated spawn region. Outside that range, players could only communicate through proximity chat.
