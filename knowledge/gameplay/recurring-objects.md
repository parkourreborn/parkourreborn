## Overview

There are many recurring instances of structures and placeholders that provide functionality to the map, many of which hold quite a significance in the game's world.

## Structures

Structures are essentially a much more significant version of normal objects, usually featuring unique properties that may be passive or activated.

 Multiple

### Dampeners
Dampeners are objects that reduce fall damage. Landing pads negate damage from any height (Note: If you're trying to survive a large height, you have to stabilize your fall beforehand.). Glass placed horizontally or on the floor reduces damage to a certain extent (press DESCEND right before landing on it, and if necessary, a second time to land on the ground beneath it).

### Landing Pads

Landing Pads are a large square covered with blue/red tarp.

### Sports Mats

Sports Mats are a piece of foam folded into a square shape.

### Horizontal Glass

Glass position parallel to the ground that reduces fall damage when fallen through with enough height.

### Climbables
Climbables are objects which can be clutched onto by pressing ASCEND near them, allowing you to [ CLIMB ] . They can be used to maintain combo, effectively pausing it while attached. Mag Rail lets players quickly ascend climbables by pressing USE AUGMENT while climbing them.

### Pipes

Pipes are metallic objects with a hollow interior varying in length.

### Ladders

Ladders are metallic objects of varying lengths, and can be found along different surfaces and angles.

### Window Cleaners

Window Cleaners are metal platforms hoisted onto building's windows through the use of elastic ropes.

### Elevator Cables

Some interior buildings such as Vertigo have elevator cables that can be climbed.

### Grapple/Swing Points
Grapple (or alternatively Swing) Points are flying white drones that emit a light buzzing noise from their rotors, along with a red light that illuminates them for easier viewing. They can be attached to using the Grappler or magnetized with the Mag Rail, allowing you to swing in a pendulum motion or launch towards it respectively.

Not all of them are drones—some are still neon red cubes, which is a placeholder model for them. Stack has a few special drones with their own design, holding objects in the sky.

### Cranes
Cranes are tall, long machines that vary in height. They serve as useful landmarks in most districts, especially the tallest ones. Cranes can be operated by climbing to the top and entering the control room. Sitting in the seat will allow players to change the crane direction from left to right.

The tallest crane in Downtown, near the Vestibule time trial

### Trivia

* Movable cranes were introduced in alpha 1.7.

* They were initially thought to be exclusive to Stack, but they appear to be operatable on all cranes.

## Placeholders

Placeholders are unique entities which are used as a way to help guide the developers when it comes to creating something new. Players can't interact with them, as they serve no functional purpose in-game.

### Dummy
## Dummy

Dummies are placeholder entities for actual NPCs, with its only purpose being to sit in one spot to help the developers design a proper NPC in its place.

### Buq0
## Buq0

Buq0's avatar, which is used as a measurement tool for Verna's size, prevents districts from looking out of proportion. He is an essential part in the building process because of this.

## Trivia

* Buq0's real name is Ben.

* Buq0 is pronounced as "Buck-O".

* Buq0 used to be housed within a random Dirwik interior until alpha 1.2, when he was moved somewhere in Fragment—as shown in the gallery below.

* As of alpha 1.3, there are no instances of Buq0 on the map.
