Lightning (Lightning789) founded the PARKOUR Reborn Wiki on December 18, 2022, first on Fandom (it moved to Miraheze in August 2025), and is its bureaucrat, spending most of the time designing and structuring the wiki. A Mongoose Studio credits Lightning as Wiki Founder, and Lightning is a Senior Moderator on the staff team. Lightning's graffiti is on a wall northeast of the Snout checkpoint.
