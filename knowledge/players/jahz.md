jahz is a time-trial runner and the best-known name in Wasans, the Parkour Reborn time-trial community. He has been #1 on the overall Wasans leaderboard for a long time, which makes him the top time-trial player in the game. His current rank and score are on the live Wasans leaderboard.

He was the first player to reach a Wasans score of 1.000, which means he held the world record on every time trial at the same time. That earned him the nickname "True Wasans". He no longer holds every world record, so his score is below 1.000 now, but he has stayed at #1.
