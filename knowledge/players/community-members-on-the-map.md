Short notes on community members, playtesters and moderators mentioned on the wiki. Many of them have their name sprayed as graffiti somewhere on the map.

## Jorben
Senior Moderator and playtester, known as "The Verna Voidwalker". Jorben's graffiti is inside a building in the southwest corner (the suburbs) of Dirwik.

## Poppy_SynQ
Senior Moderator and playtester. Poppy recorded and uploaded hudzell's Twitch Q&A. Poppy's avatar is painted in an interior at the Reflective Bridging checkpoint on the east edge of Fragment.

## Ziron
Senior Moderator and playtester. Ziron's graffiti is southeast of the Stop checkpoint.

## Graphix
Moderator and playtester. Graphix's graffiti is just east of Mantra's Hideout and north of Apollo.

## Shifters
Moderator and playtester. Shifter's graffiti is on a building along the Neon Bold time trial route.

## Spugg
Former Moderator and playtester. Spugg's graffiti is next to the crane.

## PurrkourCat
Playtester. PurrkourCat's graffiti is on a building north of Apollo.

## Chroma
Playtester. Chroma's graffiti is on a building east of Downtown Athletics.

## Blanket
Playtester. Blanket's graffiti is on the side of Legacy Foundation.

## Alvernerd
Playtester. Alver's graffiti is on a building wall between PRK Tech and Celestial.

## Craft_R
Made the artwork of A Mongoose Studio's developers and testers in the garage of Oasis. Craft_R's name is inside the Number One checkpoint building, across from Mike.
