Buq0 (pronounced "Buck-O", real name Ben) is the Lead World Designer at A Mongoose Studio.

* Buq0's avatar is used as a measuring tool when building Verna, so districts stay in proportion, which made him an essential part of the building process.
* Copies of his avatar used to be hidden on the map: in a Dirwik interior until alpha 1.2, then in Fragment (along the Transitional Buildings and at leftover work-in-progress props near Downtown). Since alpha 1.3 there are none left on the map.
* Buq0's graffiti is inside a concrete frame building right behind GL.
