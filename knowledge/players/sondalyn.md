Sondalyn is a developer at A Mongoose Studio, working as Project Manager and Concept Artist, and is also a Manager and Senior Moderator on the staff team.

* Drew the Springhook concept art, early Stack concept art, and the artwork Neon was announced with.
* Sondalyn's graffiti is at the Sonder checkpoint, west of Apollo, and one of the community artworks on the map is by Sondalyn.
