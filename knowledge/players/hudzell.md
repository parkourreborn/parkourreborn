hudzell is the creator of PARKOUR Reborn and the owner of A Mongoose Studio, the studio that develops the game. His role at the studio is Creative Director and Lead Designer.

## Background
* hudzell also created PARKOUR Legacy, the game Reborn is a remake of. Legacy was made under the name Parkour Dev, which hudzell later rebranded as A Mongoose Studio (founded under the new name on April 14, 2023; the team itself goes back to around 2016).
* Reborn started development on February 8, 2022, and first released (pre-alpha) on November 3, 2023.
* He is on X/Twitter as hudzellRBX and has a YouTube channel, which hosts one of the first introductory videos for Reborn.
* He has the Roblox Studio badge in-game.

## Streams and Announcements
* hudzell used to run developer livestreams on Twitch and Discord (bi-weekly Twitch streams), where he shows upcoming content and answers questions. Much of what is known about upcoming content comes from these streams.
* Mag Rail was first teased as a "secret gear" in one of his livestreams.
* He teased the Glove for alpha 1.1 with a screenshot posted in the game's Discord.
* He has said Reborn will never get VR support, because it is nearly impossible with how the game is designed and would waste development resources.

## In-Game References
* hudzell's graffiti is at the top of Vertigo.
* There is a checkpoint called "hudzell Fall Spot".
* The developer console prints "hudzell's great pain reduced your time by ..." after you finish a time trial or challenge.
* He added the famous cheese wedge because he saw a player standing at that spot and decided to put a cheese wedge exactly there.
* Technically, a close friend of hudzell, has an avatar on the rooftop of Legacy Foundation.
