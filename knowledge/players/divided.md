Divided is the Sound Designer and a Content Creator at A Mongoose Studio, and an Engineer on the staff team.

* Probably animated the first Stack teasers and trailers.
* On January 13, 2024 he uploaded a YouTube video claiming to show how to get the Swing Grappler. It was a troll, and became possibly the biggest community gaslight in PARKOUR history.
* Divided's loadout has a rare chance of showing up when you open your loadout from the Tab menu.
* His graffiti is on the Number One checkpoint building (where Crystal ends), and mini avatars of spwb and Divided sit on the crane in front of Vertigo.
