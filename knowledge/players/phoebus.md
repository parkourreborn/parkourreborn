Phoebus is a former Wiki Admin and Bug Manager credited by A Mongoose Studio, and a former Senior Moderator. Phoebus joined the wiki staff in early 2024, helped massively with organizing and adding content, made the wiki's Reborn logo variant, and stepped down after about 14 months. Phoebus's graffiti is on the bottom floor of Apollo, a nod to the full name "Phoebus Apollo".
