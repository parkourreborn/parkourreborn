## Overview

Corporations are recurring entities in the game's world, usually identified by recognizable signs on their branded buildings. Presumably, they function as real life businesses.

## GL

### Company
## Info
* Type: Site (Corporation)
* Located: Downtown, GL Tower, Fragment, Northwest corner of the district

## Overview

GL is represented by two, giant neon letters. It seems to be a more secretive organization, as hinted at by Derek's dialogue during his questline for the Binoculars.

## GL Tower

### Type

Site (Tower)

### Located

West edge of Downtown

GL Tower is a unique tower consisting of a blue gridded window-like pattern, with the letters GL displayed on its exterior in neon white material. Adjacent to it is a building with a respawn transceiver on top of it.

#### Trivia

* Prior to alpha 1.73, this tower was only climbable from the outside.

* At this point, the tower didn't hold much significance, and was quite hard to scale without being proficient in movement

* The transceiver used to be nearby on an adjacent building. It is now on top of GL Tower itself.

### Construction
## Dirwik Construction

### Type

Site (Building)

### Located

South edge of Dirwik

Dirwik Construction is a massive, multi-layered construction site featuring many building materials, including large cargo crates and long steel beams; most of the other materials are usually made up of wood, as shown by the abundance of wooden pallets everywhere.

#### Trivia

* Dirwik Construction used to be a lot smaller prior to alpha 1.2, when it got a massive upgrade.

* Previously, the 'grappler housing' resource (now known as the 'winch mount') was located on one of the wooden pallets, before being moved to Petra's shop.

* It received a minor interior in alpha 1.52.

### Resources Branch
## Resources Branch

### Type

Site (Extension)

### Located

Across from Celestial

Resources Branch is an extension of Dirwik Construction. As the name implies, it is the resource branch for Dirwik Construction, housing extra materials for the site, and likely providing a rest area for workers inside. It also has a small balcony outside.

* Resources Branch is one of the few sites which are split into multiple buildings.

### Offices
## Dirwik Offices

### Type

Site (Building)

### Located

North from Dirwik Construction

Dirwik Offices is an extremely tall, minimalistic office building, having only a neon sign displayed upfront with its name, and a small nook along the half-way point to the top.

#### Trivia

* Dirwik Offices can only be scaled through the use of a zipline near Artemis, making it one of the more harder to scale obscure buildings alongside towers like GL's.

### PRK Industries
## PRK Industries

### Type

Site (Building)

### Located

Northwest corner of the district

PRK Industries is a factory-style building located in the northwest corner of the district. Inside, there is a storage room filled with building materials and cargo crates. The main factory area consists of pipes arranged throughout the space, more boxes and crates, landing pads, and various construction equipment. The 'bounding fluctuator' can be found near the top of this area.

#### Trivia

* PRK Industries was added in alpha 1.5, and is presumed to be part of the other Dirwik businesses.

* PRK Industries being a factory-style building is because of how close it is to the district Stack.
