## Stack Interior Hideout

### Type

Site (Hideout/Interior)

### Located

Dirwik and Stack edge

An interior space was added in alpha 1.73, and appears to be a hangout area of sorts. The inside matches with Stack's theme, featuring cement and brick walls, some wooden shelving and tables, and shelves with boxes. There are a few other props scattered around, such as some resources that are normally collected around the map. The area has multiple floors, and appears to be more barren then many other hideouts scattered around the map. There's also a one-way exit door at the top, just like the one in Artemis.

#### Trivia

* This area is one of the very few locations that makes use of a one-way fire exit.
