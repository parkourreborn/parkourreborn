## Fragment

### Aven
## Info
* Type: Site
* Located: In between southern and northern parts of Fragment; Ascension time trial.

## Overview

Aven is a tall building located between the northern (time square area) and southern part of Fragment. Its name can be seen along the exterior in neon red. The site consists of an interior with two floors, with tables and chairs being distributed all over the second floor and rooftop. Aven is where the NPC Harper is located, and it also houses the Ascension time trial, which can be accessed from the roof of a section of the building.
