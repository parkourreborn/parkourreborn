## Overview

Stack is a district within the map of the game. It is the fourth one to be added overall, and the second one to be added during the alpha stage. Stack is an industrial themed district with a rustic atmosphere. It consists of construction sites, smokestacks, and various props that fit its factory-like theme. Stack's color palette is made up of low saturated colors and materials (gray, brown, cement), contrasted with pink neon lights scattered throughout.

## Trivia

* Stack has two videos dedicated to it on the A Mongoose Studio YouTube account.

* Its the first teasers and trailers to be animated, presumably by Divided.

* It is the biggest district in the game as of alpha 1.7.

* Stack features unique cranes.

* They are shaped and colored differently than the ones in other districts, such as Downtown and Dirwik. They are darker, and have two poles instead of the usual tower apex.

* Stack also has the most cranes out of any district, with a total of 11.

* Stack has been reworked from when it was first revealed.

* This makes it the second district in total to be reworked, with the first being Fragment.

* Stack has many props that are unique to it.

* Stack was originally planned to release with beta, but instead came earlier.

* Stack's challenge tower is confirmed to be called Foundry, and is currently in the works.

* It has taken longer than expected since the developers want to add new exciting features to the tower.

* In one of hudzell’s developer livestreams, the idea was mentioned that Stack’s challenge tower could be called "Overflow".

* Stack has a predominately dull brown and neon pink color palette. Despite this, a small section of Stack directly behind Zephyr consists of a white, blue, and green color palette, matching the tower and contrasting the main district's colors.

* Stack has the least number of checkpoints (31) as of alpha 1.7.

* This also makes Stack the most sparsely checkpointed district in the map, as it is the largest district, yet has the fewest checkpoints.

* Stack received many renovations and environment changes in alpha 1.72. This included some new props, texture changes, a few more checkpoints, and structural changes to buildings.
