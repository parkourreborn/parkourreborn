## Overview

Downtown is a district within the map of the game. It is the first one to be added overall, and was released along with the pre-alpha stage. Downtown is an urban area home to the essence of parkour, with its "dynamic playground like environment" and "various challenges." It has many features, such as towering rooftops, cluttered office interiors, and nods to Legacy, providing a challenge even to more experienced players.

## Trivia

* Downtown has one video dedicated to it on the A Mongoose Studio YouTube account.

* Downtown is where players spawn in when they first join the game, as it houses the Tutorial.

* Downtown will be the first entirely completed district, having only a bit of progress left.

* Downtown was the first district to have its title animated. The titles either haven't been animated yet, or were animated later.

* Downtown is the only district that gives 10 rank stars when entering it; the rest only give 5.

* Downtown has the most checkpoints out of any district (53).

* Downtown borders Fragment to the east, Stack to the north, Dirwik to the west, and Skyarc (unreleased) to the south.

## Quick Facts

Downtown is the first district added to the game and was released with the pre-alpha stage.

It is an urban area focused on the essence of parkour, with a dynamic playground-like environment, many rooftops, office interiors, and references to PARKOUR Legacy.

Downtown is where players first spawn because it contains the Tutorial.
