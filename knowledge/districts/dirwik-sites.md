### Apartments
## Info
* Type: Site (Building)
* Located: In the vicinity of The Highpoint

## Overview

Apartments are a set of multiple separate buildings within Dirwik used as living space for civilians. In the future, they might be rentable by players at the cost of credits or tokens.

### Runaways Hideout
## Runaways Hideout

### Type

Site (Interior)

### Located

Under Nimbus' extension building

The Runaways Hideout is a small space underneath one of the Nimbus buildings. This area is related to some of the easter egg notes scattered around the map, with many of the Runaways related notes being in Dirwik. The space is decorated with some graffiti, spray cans, and seating. Runaways is presumably a group of runners who use the city as their canvas for graffiti.

#### Trivia

* This area was added in alpha 1.5.

### The Highpoint
## The Highpoint

### Type

Site (Café)

### Located

Directly underneath Artemis

The Highpoint is a rooftop café consisting of multiple chairs and tables for loitering, lights, a bar area, and an interior kitchen. The café's name is clearly displayed along the outside exterior, and underneath it is the phrase "C'MON UP!".

## Trivia

* Prior to alpha 1.3, the café did not have an official name, was slightly more empty, and had no interior kitchen.

* The 'override module' resource used to be at the The Highpoint resting on the bar area's counter, and is now purchasable from Petra as of alpha 1.2.

* The Highpoint is one of the locations players will travel to while completing Derek's questline.

* In his questline dialog, he mentions that they don't like people "outstaying their welcome," suggesting they prefer visitors to leave sooner rather than later.

* It houses one of the easter egg sticky notes inside the kitchen.

### Dirwik Coffee Co. and Hideout
## Dirwik Coffee Co.

### Type

Site (Café)

### Located

At the Wall Climb Boost challenge

Dirwik Coffee Co. is a coffee shop located right next to the Wall Climb Boost challenge. The interior features typical coffee shop furniture and props, including seating, donuts, coffee, and a menu on its wall. A few bookshelves are placed against the wall, with one of them acting like a door, leading to one of many hideouts in the game.

The hideout is underneath the shop, and is also accessible via a vent and small space right next to the Wallclimb Boost challenge. It has a cozy atmosphere, featuring beanbag chairs, board game props, and some electronic devices. It appears to be more of an improvised and resourceful setup, with boxes and large wooden cargo stacked and scattered around.

## Trivia

* This area is one of the many hideouts added in alpha 1.73.

* It's one of the sites that introduces some unique props that weren't in the game prior.

* The second café to be featured in Dirwik, with the first one being The Highpoint.
