## Overview

Dirwik is a district within the map of the game. It is the second one to be added overall, as well as during the pre-alpha stage. Dirwik is dominated by large buildings with a darker color-palette than most other districts, and has a unique futuristic atmosphere; this is especially true during the night, when Dirwik becomes notably more dim while being lit up by the neon blue lights scattered throughout the district.

## Trivia

* Dirwik has one video dedicated to it on the A Mongoose Studio YouTube account.

* Dirwik was released on November 20th, 2023 as part of pre-alpha 1.4.

* Dirwik currently has the most Easter Eggs out of any district.

* Dirwik is currently the only district with its own corporation.

* Dirwik received various environment changes in alpha 1.5, especially around the Neon Bold time trial area and the southern suburban area.

* Upon release, Dirwik was in an unfinished state, with windowless concrete towers, but has since been receiving transformations to make it live up to its expectations.

* Dirwik is one of the two districts not bordering Fragment, the other being Asylum.
