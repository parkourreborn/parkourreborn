## Downtown Athletics

### Type

Site (Interior)

### Located

South of the Tutorial

Downtown Athletics is a gym containing various workout equipment and exercise spots, such as dumbbells, sports mats, and monkey bars.

#### Trivia

* Downtown Athletics is the largest single-floored interior so far.

* It was previously just called 'Gym' before alpha 1.5.

* The wall run spot to the left of the entrance originally had three walls instead of the current two. This was the case even before the Drifter Glove was introduced.

### Old Tutorial
## Old Tutorial

### Type

Site (Structure)

### Located

Center of Downtown

The old Tutorial guided players through basic movements prior to being replaced in alpha 1.6. It is located next to the current interior one, and consists of many floors within a tall concrete structure. It can still be accessed by foot, and the Genesis time trial goes directly through it.

#### Trivia

* The old Tutorial was replaced in alpha 1.6.

* The original tutorial start was placed in a different interior that lead out towards the concrete structure. It has been closed off since.

* There used to be an old Tutorial walkthrough on the A Mongoose Studio YouTube.

* The coil ability book used to be at the old tutorial building. It was moved next to Cool Spot checkpoint in alpha 1.6.

### Crystal Interior
## Crystal Interior

### Type

Site (Hideout/Interior)

### Located

Crystal time trial

The Crystal Interior refers to an indoor space at the Crystal time trial. It appears to be a lounging/hideout area for players in the area, and features general furniture (couch, television, seating) and decorative props.

#### Trivia

* Some players may consider this interior one of the more significant additions of alpha 1.73, as it's located near spawn and the Crystal time trial, a popular area.
