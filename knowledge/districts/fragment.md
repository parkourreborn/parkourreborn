## Overview

Fragment is a district within the map of the game. It is the third one to be added overall, and the first during the alpha stage. Fragment is a modern-day business district characterized by corporate office buildings and sleek skyscrapers that tower over players, with many explorable interiors. Some of the buildings within Fragment appear to be under renovation, as indicated by the red/blue scaffolding surrounding them, along with various construction-related equipment, such as standing ladders.

## Highrise

Highrise is a sector within Fragment, making up 20% of the district. It contains Fragment's challenge tower, Zephyr, which is currently the tallest tower in the game as of alpha 1.5. Highrise is located towards the northern part of the district, and is made up of multiple interconnected buildings.

The exterior of Highrise consists of tall modern buildings made up of white concrete, glass, and a blue/green color scheme. There are long vertical billboards placed throughout, along with Zephyr's name displayed in both English and Greek letters.

Fragment's third respawn transceiver is located here, and players will have to climb to the top of Zephyr to reach it.

## Trivia

* Fragment has one video dedicated to it on the A Mongoose Studio YouTube account.

* Fragment was released on August 30th, 2024 at 5 PM EST during alpha 1.3.

* Fragment has the most amount of interiors (19) out of every district, giving it one of the largest explorable plains.

* Many of the interiors were inaccessible when alpha 1.3 came out, since the builders were focusing on completing the exteriors first.

* Fragment's Highrise sector is entirely comprised of its challenge tower Zephyr, going through multiple different buildings to reach the top.

* Previously, it was common to refer to "Highrise" as the challenge tower of Fragment. After the release of alpha 1.5, it become more clear that Zephyr is the designated name for the tower, which is located within Highrise.

* Fragment is likely the city's center of business, trade, and finance due to its abundance of offices. It's also adjacent to all but 2 districts (Asylum and Dirwik), making it a hotspot for large Corporations to settle in.

* Fragment's third teaser image, shown in the gallery below, is one of the very few to showcase multiple districts at once, those being Fragment itself and Skyarc.

* A common misconception about Fragment is that it is a nod to Legacy. This is in fact false, and was only a rumor that occurred due to certain similarities between Fragment and Treximea (Legacy's city).

* There is, however, a building named "Legacy Foundation" within the district.

* Fragment has a few large vents that lift you upwards, similar to the ones introduced in Stack. One of them is inbetween the Paneful Pulp and Heartbreak checkpoints. The other known was is south of the Nest checkpoint.

* Fragment is so far the only district to have no cranes.

* Fragment received an animated district title in alpha 1.72, making it the second district to get one after Downtown.

* Fragment will be receiving another revamp in a future update, making it approximately the third time it has been reworked/overhauled in some way.
