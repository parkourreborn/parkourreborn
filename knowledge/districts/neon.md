Neon is a shelved project for the game. It might return in the future, but that is not guaranteed.

## Info
* Type: DLC (City)

## Overview

Neon was a newly announced city coming to both Reborn and Legacy. The city was a DLC, meaning it would have been purchasable as a gamepass, and accessible in both games. Because it was a DLC, it would have been an entirely separate city from the base Reborn city (Verna), and was planned to be approximately 3/4ths the size of Treximea (Legacy's city). There would have been some slight differences between the Legacy and Reborn version of Neon, with adjustments to interiors and other areas to accommodate for the different capabilities of each game.

Neon would not have been available to play during the beta or alpha stages of Reborn, and would have instead come out during its full release.

### The Pier

One of the districts within Neon was known as 'The Pier.' This location was "abandoned in the middle of remodeling due to economic hardships faced by the city." This area was planned to be more of a social area, with slower-paced and technical play closer to the ground.

## Trivia

* Neon was introduced with artwork made by Sondalyn, which can be viewed in the gallery below.

* Although Neon's cancellation was confirmed by zAdamBomb, it is more so shelved; this means it can be picked back up again sometime in the near future, but isn't guaranteed to happen.
