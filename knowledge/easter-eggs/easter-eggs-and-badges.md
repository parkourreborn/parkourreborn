Easter Eggs are hidden references scattered around the map with a slightly different style compared to their counterparts. Some are more significant than others, such as the graffiti showcasing an important community figure's name in-game.

Badges are achievements awarded to players for completing specific tasks, reaching milestones, or discovering secrets within the game.

## User Interface
* Divided's loadout: Has a rare chance of occurring when opening your loadout.
* we'll get 'em next time: Has a rare chance of occurring when failing a delivery mission.
* Loneliness - or tranquility?: Appears in the playerlist under the player's name when they're alone in a server.

### Party Invites
* does not fix w randos: Has a 1% chance of occurring when trying to invite someone with their party invites set to 'Friends.' (while not being their friend)
* on their loner arc: Has a 1% chance of occurring when trying to invite someone with their party invites set to 'Nobody.'

### Developer Console
* hudzell's great pain: Outputs after completing a time trial or challenge (.000xxxxx, essentially the visual that's normally displayed).
* powersliden't: Outputted whenever your powerslide ran out. No longer in the game.

## Trivia
* Gish used to be the only collideable moving object in the map up until alpha 1.12. Since then, the radio has been introduced.
* hudzell added the cheese wedge because he saw somebody stand there, and thought "Why not add a cheese wedge directly where that player is standing at?"
* The golden vent is likely a reference to the one from Legacy.
 * Many high tier valuables spawn around it.
* The neon red orb is nicknamed "Rorb" by the community.
* The Checkpoint Community's graffiti logo used to have a translucent version, which got replaced with a graffiti of a person smiling.
