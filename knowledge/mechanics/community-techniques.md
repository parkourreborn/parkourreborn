This is a sub-page under movement where anybody can add techniques they discover while playing the game.
 Please view the guidelines and notice below before submitting a technique of your own.

## NOTICE

This page is VERY EXPERIMENTAL. You may see drastic changes to the way this page is formatted and its functionality over time. Everything is subject to change or be removed. Please understand that this is more of a testing phase to figure out how we can incorporate community techniques to be added by everyone on the wiki, rather than be hand selected by Staff.

Anything added to this page is UNOFFICIAL, but may be moved to the official page if it is later decided by staff. If it is officially recognized by A Mongoose Studio as a technique, it will automatically be put in the official page regardless of its factors.

### GUIDELINES

* A technique should be unique, and serve some sort of purpose.

* A technique should do something that abilities can't accomplish on their own.

* A technique should NOT be a bug. Make sure that is utilizes intended mechanics of the game.

* Follow the wiki's Rules & Guidelines.
 Guidelines are subject to be changed/updated

### HOW DO I ADD TECHNIQUES TO THIS PAGE?

* Click the add button below.

* Copy and paste a technique already in the page, and change the values. Use the comments to guide you.

* FREQUENTLY PREVIEW YOUR WORK: Use the Show Preview button to make sure everything looks right (or ALT + SHIFT + P).

If you need help, don't hesitate to contact a staff member, or leave a comment.
