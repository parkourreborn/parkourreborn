There are various types of movements mechanics within the game. Abilities are unlocked by finding and completing challenges scattered around the map. Each one has its own strengths in different situations. Additionally, there is a multitude of community-made movement techniques, which utilize a combination of abilities. There are also many tips & tricks made to aid you in traversing around the map.

## Mechanics

| MECHANIC | DESCRIPTION |
| --- | --- |
| Momentum | Running allows you to build momentum, represented by the blue bar. Momentum affects your run speed, jump height, wall run speed, wall climb height, etc. Turning too sharply will slightly impact your momentum. Leveling up increases your momentum cap, up to level 20. Momentum can be built through some other means, such as riding a zipline with a Zipline Hook. |
| Temporary Momentum | Temporary momentum can be built up through means such as dashing and performing actions which would normally grant you "blue" Momentum, represented by the red section of the Momentum bar. It is lost upon touching the ground, but can be extended for a bit if you jump (can only be done once per chain jump), or when your dash ends. Temporary momentum can go past your level's momentum cap, but is still hard-capped at the speed you would get from maximum momentum. Dashing adds a bit of temporary momentum, but prevents momentum from building up any further. Afterboost doubles the temporary momentum gained from dashing for a very short period of time, and can be used as a last boost since it can be activated right as dash is about to run out. |
| Velocity | Velocity is separate from momentum, and dictates how fast and in what direction you're currently moving. There are ways you can increase your velocity without increasing your momentum, and vice-versa. Velocity doesn't affect the strength of movement mechanics, unlike momentum. |
| Terminal Velocity | Terminal velocity is the state in which you begin to spin out-of-control, and cannot survive a fall through rolling or clutching. To stop yourself from spinning, you must perform a fall stabilization; stabilizing your fall is essential for surviving long falls onto landing pads or sports mats. |
| Bhopping | When landing on the ground, you get 100ms of air-friction instead of ground-friction, which allows you to maintain your velocity more easily by repeatedly jumping. |
| Attack | ATTACK is the keybind that allows you to perform every combat ability (Dropkick is currently the only one). By default, it's binded to Left Click/M1 for keyboard and mouse users, and can be changed in the settings menu. |

## Abilities

Not to be confused with combat abilities.
 Obtained via books and challenges.

### Coil
* Input: DESCEND (Midair)
* Raises your hitbox slightly, allowing you to clear obstacles and slightly increasing your upwards reach.
Obtained at Level 2.

### Dash
* Input: DASH TAP or HOLD (Keybind)
* Increases acceleration and grants bonus Momentum for up to 2 seconds. Jumping off a ledge while dashing will increase jump distance and speed. Performing a precise landing while Dash is on cooldown instantly resets it.
* How to get it: Challenge: Dash Training Located northeast of the Riser time trial, towards the edge of Downtown.

### Powerslide
* Input: DESCEND Hold (Preserves Chain)
* Boosts speed momentarily. Powersliding towards a low obstacle will cause you to slide underneath it.
* How to get it: Challenge: The Art of Powerslide South of the building where Solar time trial is found

### Dropdown
* Input: DESCEND (Preserves Chain)
* Powersliding towards a ledge will activate Dropdown. Slightly reduces fall damage, increasing maximum fall distance. Powersliding off a ledge at high speeds will not activate Dropdown.
* How to get it: Challenge: Dropper North of Apollo

### Afterboost
* Input: DASH Hold to charge ➜ RELEASE ➜ DASH TAP
* Afterboost allows you to access long jump, but can be useful on its own. To activate Afterboost, charge your dash until it's full. When you release your dash, your dash bar will turn red. While it's red, if you press dash down again, you will get a minor velocity boost, and activate Afterboost for a short moment. While Afterboost is active, jumping off a ledge will cause you to long jump. Activating Afterboost gives you a boost in momentum, represented by the orange section of your momentum bar. This bonus momentum will persist until you touch the ground again.
* How to get it: Challenge: A Longer Jump South of The Art of Powerslide starting point

### Wall Boost
* Input: [ AIR DASH ] ➜ ASCEND x2 DOUBLE TAP
* Holding Dash midair will activate "air dash". While air dashing, look upwards and double-tap Ascend against a wall to activate Wall Boost. Grants significant upwards velocity, allowing for greater speed and efficiency than a regular wall climb.
* How to get it: Challenge: Wall Climb Boost Southwest from Advancement Beta

### Wall Kick
* Input: [ AIR DASH ] ➜ ASCEND (Back against an object + Midair)
* Holding Dash midair will activate "air dash". While air dashing, face your back to a wall and tap Ascend. Kicks off the wall, redirecting you towards the direction you are looking.
* How to get it: Challenge: Leg Day On the roof of Nimbus

### Chain
* Input: ASCEND TAP or HOLD
* Long Jump to begin a chain, indicated by the red outline around your momentum bar. While a chain is active, landing and quickly jumping off another ledge will cause you to Long Jump once more, continuing the chain. Speed and distance increases with each subsequent chain jump. Moves like powerslide and dropdown preserve your chain.
* How to get it: At the top of Vertigo

## Combat

### Dropkick
* Input: [ COIL ] ➜ ATTACK (Midair)
* Coil and press <Attack> to dropkick. Knocks players back based on your velocity. Dropkick stops upwards movement, and slightly redirects if forward. It can also be used while swinging to redirect your trajectory. Landing while dropkicking briefly immobilizes you, but you can Powerslide out of it.
Obtained at Level 5.

## Other

### Combo
* Input: View Full Page
* A combo will begin automatically as you parkour. Going to areas you have not yet been during the combo will extend your combo's "coverage". The faster you extend your coverage, the higher your score will be. You will be granted XP at the end of a combo based on your total score.
Obtained at Level 10.

## Basic

### High Jump
* Input: ASCEND HOLD
* High Jump is pretty simple, holding down jump allows you to jump higher by altering your gravity.

### Vault
* Input: ASCEND TAP or HOLD
* Vaulting an object is a quick way to smoothly pass over it. While vaulting, you can hold jump for a powerful vault jump. If you release your movement input while holding space, you will springboard instead.

### Mantle
* Input: ASCEND
* To mantle a ledge instead of normally climbing it, your head must be over the edge. Mantling allows you to springboard, and is faster than a normal ledge climb.

### Springboard
* Input: ASCEND HOLD (Release other movement keys)
* Springboard is a powerful vertical jump, and can be done out of either a mantle or a vault. To springboard, release your movement input and hold space during a mantle or a vault.

### Wall Climb
* Input: ASCEND HOLD
* The Wall Climb is an essential movement for vertical traversal. By default, you can wall climb only once until you touch the ground again. If you're falling too fast, you will not be able to grip the wall to climb it. The height you climb depends on your Momentum. Pressing DESCEND during a wall climb stops it. The reticle turns gray when you begin to fall, indicating you can't wall climb.

### Wall Run
* Input: FORWARD / BACKWARD + ASCEND HOLD
* The Wall Run is an essential movement for horizontal traversal. By default, you can wall run only twice until you touch the ground again. To use the second wall run, you need to wall run on a surface with a different angle from the last. The speed you run along the wall depends on your Momentum. By default, releasing the jump button allows you to jump out of the wall run, and will redirect your velocity forwards. Tapping DESCEND during a wall run will cut it short. Your wall run ammo will start flashing when it's about to run out.

### Wall Jump
* Input: ASCEND
* Wall Jump is a blanket term used to refer to any jump coming out of a wall climb or wall run. Wall run jumps are generally considered more powerful than wall climb jumps, however both have their use cases. Visit the page for Wall Jump or Wall Run for more specific information.

### Slide Not To Be Confused With Powerslide
* Input: DESCEND Hold
* A slide happens when you hold DESCEND on a slope, while under an object, or walk on a very steep slope.You can also perform a slide by landing while coiling.

### Air Dash
* Input: DASH TAP or HOLD (Midair)
* Air dashing is indicated by a purple bar above the Momentum bar, and will appear when the DASH button is held while airborne.

### Climb
* Input: FORWARDS or BACKWARDS
* When clutching onto a climbable object, you can climb upwards by holding [ FORWARDS ] and downwards by holding [ BACKWARDS ] .

### Climb (Leap)
* Input: ASCEND
* While climbing, you can leap upwards using ASCEND . There's a short cooldown before you can perform it again.

### Climb (Jump)
* Input: ASCEND
* While climbing, you can jump off using ASCEND . If you are facing towards the object you are climbing, you will instead do a [ CLIMB (LEAP) ] .

### Flail Jump
* Input: DASH ➜ ASCEND (At an edge)
* A flail jump is performed similarly to a long jump: by holding DASH and pressing ASCEND at an edge. The distance gained is further than a normal jump, but not as far as a long jump.

### Impact
* Input: DESCEND (Before landing)
* When you are about to land, press DESCEND to reduce fall damage. Depending on whether or not you were moving, the impact animation will change.

### Fall Stabilization
* Input: DESCEND HOLD + ASCEND HOLD (Midair)
* While falling, you can hold down ASCEND and DESCEND to prevent spinning out of control.

## Intermediate

### Long Jump
* Input: [ AFTERBOOST ] ➜ ASCEND (At an edge)
* Long Jump is a very powerful horizontal jump. To Long Jump, activate afterboost and quickly jump off a ledge. You can combine this ability with High Jump for even more distance. Your velocity will become "committed" as it goes past your Momentum, causing you to lose air control unless explicitly altered by movement inputs. Meaning, look before you leap!

### Wall Clutch
* Input: [ AIR DASH ] ➜ ASCEND
* Wall Clutch allows you to suspend yourself on a wall for a short period of time. Unlike a wall climb, this can be performed while falling. To perform it, hold DASH midair to activate [ AIR DASH ] . At this point, pressing ASCEND on a wall will activate wall clutch instead of wall climbing. This movement is what enables wall boosting.

### Wall Bounce
* Input: ASCEND TAP
* When facing a wall, tapping ASCEND midair and without pressing any movement keys will cause you to bounce backwards. Quickly flicking in a direction allows you to redirect your velocity towards it. You can also perform it by quickly exiting a wall climb (though the timing is tight).

## Advanced

### Coyote Time
* Input: [ PASSIVE ]
* Coyote Time is a passive ability which essentially makes you act like you're on ground when you're actually midair. It's triggered whenever you leave the ground without pressing ASCEND . Jumping during coyote time counts as you being on an edge, meaning afterboost will result in a long jump regardless of when you entered coyote time.

### Jump Boost
* Input: [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ WALL BOOST ] ➜ [ HIGH JUMP ] OR ASCEND
* A Jump Boost is when you jump along the edge of an object with significant upwards velocity, compounding your jump force with the velocity you already have.

## Other

### Ledge Grab
* Input: ASCEND While ascending or after a Pipe Climb
* You clump your hands together to grab onto the ledge above you and raise your entire body onto it.

### Wide Ledge Grab
* Input: ASCEND While falling or after a Wall Climb
* You extend your hands to your shoulders and grab onto the ledge above you, raising your entire body onto it.

### Barrier Vault
* Input: ASCEND While near or climbing onto a thin object
* You grab onto the thin object's top side which you are climbing on and raise your body in a vault-like motion over it.

## Removed

### Slide Boost
* Input: [ SLIDE ] ➜ ASCEND TAP or HOLD
* Slide boosting was performed by sliding after falling from a short height then jumping out of it within a small time frame. The boost in velocity you get depends on how far you fell before initiating it.

## Notes

* Additive Jump Velocity is the term used for when a jump's velocity gets added on to by another move.

* Try experimenting with techniques yourself; most, but not all variants may be showcased on this page.

## Gearless

### Wumpy (aka Long Jump Boost)
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ [ WALL BOOST ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Wumpy seamlessly combines long jumping, wall boosting, and chaining to perform a large leap. Start by long jumping towards a wall, then wall boosting. Once the player just barely clears the edge of an object/building, they will trigger coyote time. Doing one last jump during this window will activate a chain. If executed correctly, the last jump from the chain will send the player flying upward and slightly outwards. This results from additive jump velocity utilizing the wall boosts velocity.

### Downshift (aka Dropdown Long Jump)
* Input: [ DROPDOWN ] ➜ [ LONG JUMP ]
* Downshift is done by performing a dropdown and a long jump simultaneously, causing both of the abilities' properties to stack. This grants fall damage reduction from the dropdown, activates a chain activation from the long jump, and preserves velocity.

### Pogo
* Input: [ WALL RUN ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ HIGH JUMP ]
* Pogo is done by timing a high jump right as a wall run touches the floor (within 100ms). This works because coyote time triggers during that moment. This is commonly performed with a long jump, allowing you to maintain a chain after wall running.

### Pogo with Long Jump
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] ➜ [ WALL RUN ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Pogo is done by timing a high jump right as a wall run touches the floor (within 100ms). This works because coyote time triggers during that moment. This is commonly performed with a long jump, allowing you to maintain a chain after wall running.

### Pipe Eject
* Input: DESCEND ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Pipe Eject is done by sliding down a climbable object until you let go of it, as that will trigger coyote time for a brief moment, therefore allowing you to chain if you had one active. It can also be done from a great height, as the velocity from falling is enough to trigger coyote time, similar to sliding down.

### Transition Boost (aka Dropdown Boost)
* Input: [ DROPDOWN ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] OR ASCEND
* Transition Boost is done by jumping during a dropdown, making use of the slight boost you gain when beginning a dropdown and maintaining it with a jump.

### Horizontal Climb Boost (aka Hcb)
* Input: [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ Look to the side
* Horizontal Climb Boost is done by utilizing the wall bounce's velocity redirection feature, allowing you to gain velocity quickly even in tight situations. This can optionally be paired with a wall run to maintain the speed.

### Double Jump
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ [ AIR DASH ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ ASCEND x2 ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Performed by double tapping space with a chain active next to a wall while also holding down air dash. The height is exactly the same as a Pogo, but the execution is entirely different.

### Gravity Kick (aka Potato Salad)
* Input: [ WALL KICK ] ➜ [ WALL RUN ]
* Gravity Kick is done by utilizing wall kick's slight velocity redirection by falling just a bit, then maintaining said velocity with a wall run.

### Bounce Kick (aka Dragon Bounce)
* Input: [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ WALL KICK ]
* Bounce Kick is done by wall kicking immediately after a wall bounce.

### Boost Kick
* Input: [ WALL BOOST ] ➜ [ WALL KICK ]
* Boost Kick is done by wall kicking immediately after a wall boost within the same air dash.

### Wall Run Chain
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ [ WALL RUN ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Wall Run Chain is done by wall running close to an edge, wall jumping, and chaining while you're in coyote time. This allows you to gain velocity quickly while still maintaining a nice flow.

### Wall Kick Chain
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ [ WALL KICK ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Wall Kick Chain is done by wall kicking at a corner, and then chaining while you're in coyote time.

## Grappler

### Grappler High Jump
* Input: USE AUGMENT ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ HIGH JUMP ]
* Grappler High Jump is done by high jumping the moment you enter coyote time after getting pulled by using grappler. If you succeed in doing this then you will utilize additive jump velocity with grappler's pull, resulting in immense vertical velocity.

### Grappler Flail Jump
* Input: USE AUGMENT ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ FLAIL JUMP ]
* Grappler Flail Jump is done by flail jumping the moment you enter coyote time after getting pulled by using grappler. If you succeed in doing this then you will utilize additive jump velocity with grappler's pull, resulting in immense vertical velocity.

### Grappler Long Jump
* Input: USE AUGMENT ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ LONG JUMP ]
* Grappler Long Jump is done by long jumping the moment you enter coyote time after getting pulled by using grappler. If you succeed in doing this then you will utilize additive jump velocity with grappler's pull, resulting in immense vertical velocity.

### Grappler Launch
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* The sequence of moves required to perform a Grappler Launch is similar to a Grappler Long Jump, with the main difference being the timing. Begin by long jumping. Then use the grappler, timing it so it pulls you just as you land on the ground. This will trigger coyote time, allowing you to do a chain jump, which sends you flying.

### Grappler Enhanced Wumpy (aka Gumpy)
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ ( [ POWERSLIDE (OPTIONAL) ] ) ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Grappler Enhanced Wumpy is done by utilizing grappler's velocity redirection pull with an upwards facing wall bounce, simulating the wall boost part in a Wumpy. This will activate coyote time, allowing you to chain and send yourself flying with the power of grappler's pull strength. Additionally, an optional powerslide can be performed right after the coyote time. Doing so and quickly jumping out of it can significantly increase the distance covered. This goes with all the other variants.

### Grappler Enhanced Wumpy - Variant 2 (aka Gumpy)
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ [ WUMPY ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ ( [ POWERSLIDE (OPTIONAL) ] ) ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* A second variant of this involves adding a Wumpy after the first long jump, increasing your height and distance.

### Grappler Enhanced Wumpy - Variant 2 + Afterboost (aka Gumpy)
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ [ WUMPY ] ➜ [ CHARGE AFTERBOOST ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ AFTERBOOST ] ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ ( [ POWERSLIDE (OPTIONAL) ] ) ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* You can combine the second variant with an afterboost for more speed and distance. After doing the first Wumpy, start charging Afterboost once you land on the ledge. Use the grappler to pull yourself up, complete the afterboost, and then execute the remaining movements.

### Grappler Enhanced Wumpy - Variant 3 (aka Gumpy)
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ [ WUMPY ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ ( [ POWERSLIDE (OPTIONAL) ] ) ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* This variant is similar to the second, except you pull yourself downwards with the grappler after the first Wumpy, and then back up immediately after, finishing the rest of the move. Doing it this way can lead to a faster Grappler Enhanced Wumpy.

### Grappler Enhanced Wumpy - Variant 3 + Afterboost (aka Gumpy)
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ [ WUMPY ] ➜ [ CHARGE AFTERBOOST ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ AFTERBOOST ] ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ ( [ POWERSLIDE (OPTIONAL) ] ) ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* And just like variant 2, this move can be combined with an afterboost after the first wumpy for more speed and distance.

### Slingshot
* Input: [ GRAPPLER LONG JUMP ] ➜ [ POWERSLIDE ] Optional ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Slingshot is done by initiating a Grappler Long Jump to gain Velocity, start a Chain, and position yourself in a way that once you reach the peak of your jump, you'll come in contact with a ledge and thus activate Coyote Time, which in turn allows you to perform a Chain Jump and effectively slingshot yourself. You can optionally perform a Powerslide during Coyote Time to enhance your Chain Jump further, however this is much harder than not doing so.

### Boing
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ USE AUGMENT Swing ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Boing is done by wall bouncing upwards with immense amounts of Velocity beforehand, then chaining after you go just a bit over the ledge so you can activate Coyote Time. Optionally, you can add a powerslide once you just clear the ledge, giving you more distance and allow you to delay your jump for some time.

### Boing with Slide
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ USE AUGMENT Swing ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ POWERSLIDE ] ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Boing is done by wall bouncing upwards with immense amounts of Velocity beforehand, then chaining after you go just a bit over the ledge so you can activate Coyote Time. Optionally, you can add a powerslide once you just clear the ledge, giving you more distance and allow you to delay your jump for some time.

### Gronkler
* Input: [ POWERSLIDE ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ ASCEND (Have speed beforehand)
* The Gronkler can be performed by having speed beforehand, then going into a slide. As you slide, grapple upwards, look in the direction you want to go, and then jump. Doing so will redirect your velocity in the direction you're looking, allowing you to maintain your momentum and speed after landing on the ground.

### Gronkler with Long Jump
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] ➜ [ POWERSLIDE ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ ASCEND (Have speed beforehand)
* The Gronkler can be performed by having speed beforehand, then going into a slide. As you slide, grapple upwards, look in the direction you want to go, and then jump. Doing so will redirect your velocity in the direction you're looking, allowing you to maintain your momentum and speed after landing on the ground.

## Launch Rail

### Mag Boing
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ ASCEND ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ ( [ POWERSLIDE (OPTIONAL) ] ) ➜ ASCEND
* A Mag Boing is similar to a Grappler Enhanced Wumpy. Begin by doing a long jump. After falling past the wall a little bit, USE AUGMENT , and then ASCEND immediately afterwards (E -> space for most people). After clearing the ledge, you can either jump right away, or perform a quick slide and jump out of it for more distance.

### Mag Boing - Variant 2
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] ➜ [ WUMPY ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ ASCEND ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ ( [ POWERSLIDE (OPTIONAL) ] ) ➜ ASCEND
* Another variant of this move is to perform a wumpy first, and then all the other moves after. This can increase height and distance.

### Mag Boost
* Input: Have speed beforehandFor example, launch out of a wall run ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ POWERSLIDE ] ➜ ASCEND
* Mag Boosting is a common Launch Rail technique that lets you gain a lot of height and cover a large amount of distance when performed correctly. Begin by having speed beforehand (in the media demonstration, the user launches out of a wall run). Once you reach a wall, USE AUGMENT immediately followed by ASCEND , sending you directly upwards (E -> space for most people). Once you just barely clear the ledge, start a powerslide and jump out of it.

### Mag Boost
* Input: [ CHARGE AFTERBOOST ] ➜ Have speed beforehand ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ AFTERBOOST ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ POWERSLIDE ] ➜ ASCEND
* Like many other techs, this one can be combined with an afterboost. Begin by charging an afterboost prior to doing the movement. Then generate speed (e.g. launching out of a wall run). Once you reach a wall, USE AUGMENT , ASCEND , then activate afterboost, sending you upwards at greater speeds. Once you just barely clear the ledge, start a powerslide and jump out of it.

### Crossdash
* Input: USE AUGMENT ➜ [ WALL RUN ]
* A Crossdash simply uses Launch Rail's bounce mechanic to redirect your velocity with a wall run. Have speed beforehand, and then move directly towards a wall. Once you reach the wall, face whatever direction you want to go in, USE AUGMENT , and then wall run immediately after.

### Backdash
* Input: Turn around ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ WALL RUN ]
* Backdashing uses the same idea, except you're redirecting your velocity in the opposite direction. After launching off of one wall, turn around, USE AUGMENT , and immediately begin a wall run on a different wall.

### Dropdash (aka Speedvault)
* Input: (Optional: Mag Bounce)For alignment ➜ [ WALL RUN ] ➜ USE AUGMENT Launch outwards ➜ USE AUGMENT Launch inwards ➜ [ POWERSLIDE ]
* A Dropdash (or Speedvault) utilizes Launch Rail's mag dash mechanic to gain rapidly speed along the edge of a building. Start by doing a wall run (optionally preceded with a mag bounce to align yourself properly). While being very close to the edge, Launch away from the wall, quickly turn back, and launch towards it. Finish the tech with a slide to maintain the velocity.

### Mag Wavedash
* Input: [ WALL RUN ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ Face the wall ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ POWERSLIDE ] ➜ ASCEND
* To perform a Mag Wavedash, start by launching out of a wall run (wall run ➜ USE AUGMENT ). Turn to face the wall while looking upwards. At this point, the steps are essentially the same as a Mag Boost. For most people, this will be E -> space, slide after just clearing the ledge, and then jump.

### Mag Wavedash + Afterboost
* Input: [ CHARGE AFTERBOOST ] ➜ [ WALL RUN ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ Face the wall ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ AFTERBOOST ] ➜ [ POWERSLIDE ] ➜ ASCEND
* The Mag Wavedash can be performed with an [ AFTERBOOST ] . Start charging it beforehand and perform the same steps. Activate the afterboost after the wall bounce.

### Mag Jump
* Input: USE AUGMENT ➜ [ POWERSLIDE ] ➜ ASCEND
* A Mag Jump is useful for efficiently getting over small objects and props (usually vents). Begin by having speed beforehand, and position yourself slightly below the object you want to speedvault over (you may need to jump up slightly to accomplish this). Then press USE AUGMENT , slide, and then jump in the direction you want to go.

### Mag Fall Cancel
* Input: USE AUGMENT ➜ USE AUGMENT
* A Mag Fall Cancel can be used to survive a fall at any height by utilizing its drone magnetization feature. Ideally, it should be on a swing point with a wall next to it, but there are other variants to this. Simply USE AUGMENT as you approach the swing point, and press USE AUGMENT again to bounce alongside that wall.

### Mag Pogo
* Input: [ WALL RUN ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ POWERSLIDE ]
* A Mag Pogo uses the same principle as a normal pogo. You can start it by launching out of a wall run (or alternatively, wall run towards the ground like a normal pogo). Once you just barely reach the ground, you should enter coyote time. During this time, press your augment button again, followed by a slide. You will know if its done correctly if your slide is done slightly above the ground.

### Mag Pogo
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] ➜ [ WALL RUN ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ USE AUGMENT ➜ [ POWERSLIDE ] ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Another variant of this is to simply perform a long jump prior to performing the steps, allowing you to utilize the chain feature after you finish sliding

### Mag Kick (aka Dragon Bounce 2.0)
* Input: Turn around ➜ USE AGUMENT ➜ [ WALL KICK ]
* A Mag Kick can be used to redirect your velocity in the opposite direction. Simply approach a wall with speed, face your back to it, USE AGUMENT and then wall kick immediately afterwards.

### Corner Cut
* Input: [ WALL RUN ] ➜ [ WALL JUMP ] ➜ [ WALL RUN ]
* Corner Cut is done by wall jumping at the very end of the object/building's corner that you're currently wall running on, while facing towards the direction that corner is wrapping around to and immediately wall running again.

### Solar Panel Long Jump
* Input: DASH ➜ [ MANTLE ] ➜ [ LONG JUMP ]
* Mantling over a solar panel causes you to enter coyote time for a brief moment, allowing you to long jump and start chaining from just a solar panel. This only works if you're facing almost directly forwards while mantling and have enough Momentum, otherwise coyote time will not trigger.

### Coil Assisted Springboard
* Input: [ HIGH JUMP ] ➜ [ COIL ] ➜ [ SPRINGBOARD ] (Decent amount of Momentum required beforehand)
* By high jumping and coiling at the same time, you will perfectly reach the height of a big vent, allowing you to mantle or springboard off of it.

### Afterboosted Vault Jump
* Input: [ VAULT JUMP ] ➜ [ AFTERBOOST ]
* Timing an afterboost so that it happens right at the start of a vault jump will exponentially increase its distance.

### Upwards Guided Wall Bounce
* Input: [ LONG JUMP ] Activates Chain ➜ [ WALL RUN ] OPTIONAL ➜ [ WALL BOUNCE ] LOOK UPWARDS ➜ [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ CHAIN ]
* Because Wall Bounce can redirect Velocity in any direction, you can use it to send yourself upwards in such a way that it'll activate Coyote Time, allowing you to Chain and maintain your Velocity while redirecting it elsewhere. You can perform this in many different scenarios, with the most common one being done by doing a Wall Run beforehand.

### Grappler Fall Extension
* Input: USE AUGMENT ➜ [ IMPACT ]
* When Grappler is used, you slow down slightly. Because fall damage is based off of velocity, using Grappler near landing allows you to survive greater falls than you normally would be able to.
