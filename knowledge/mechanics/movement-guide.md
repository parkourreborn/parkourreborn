There are various types of movements mechanics within the game. Abilities are unlocked by finding and completing challenges scattered around the map. Each one has its own strengths in different situations. Additionally, there is a multitude of community-made movement techniques, which utilize a combination of abilities. There are also many tips & tricks made to aid you in traversing around the map.

## Mechanics

| MECHANIC | DESCRIPTION |
| --- | --- |
| Momentum | Running allows you to build momentum, represented by the blue bar. Momentum affects your run speed, jump height, wall run speed, wall climb height, etc. Turning too sharply will slightly impact your momentum. Leveling up increases your momentum cap, up to level 20. Momentum can be built through some other means, such as riding a zipline with a Zipline Hook. |
| Temporary Momentum | Temporary momentum can be built up through means such as dashing and performing actions which would normally grant you "blue" Momentum, represented by the red section of the Momentum bar. It is lost upon touching the ground, but can be extended for a bit if you jump (can only be done once per chain jump), or when your dash ends. Temporary momentum can go past your level's momentum cap, but is still hard-capped at the speed you would get from maximum momentum. Dashing adds a bit of temporary momentum, but prevents momentum from building up any further. Afterboost doubles the temporary momentum gained from dashing for a very short period of time, and can be used as a last boost since it can be activated right as dash is about to run out. |
| Velocity | Velocity is separate from momentum, and dictates how fast and in what direction you're currently moving. There are ways you can increase your velocity without increasing your momentum, and vice-versa. Velocity doesn't affect the strength of movement mechanics, unlike momentum. |
| Terminal Velocity | Terminal velocity is the state in which you begin to spin out-of-control, and cannot survive a fall through rolling or clutching. To stop yourself from spinning, you must perform a fall stabilization; stabilizing your fall is essential for surviving long falls onto landing pads or sports mats. |
| Bhopping | When landing on the ground, you get 100ms of air-friction instead of ground-friction, which allows you to maintain your velocity more easily by repeatedly jumping. |
| Attack | ATTACK is the keybind that allows you to perform every combat ability (Dropkick is currently the only one). By default, it's binded to Left Click/M1 for keyboard and mouse users, and can be changed in the settings menu. |

## Abilities

Not to be confused with combat abilities.
 Obtained via books and challenges.

### Coil
* Input: DESCEND (Midair)
* Raises your hitbox slightly, allowing you to clear obstacles and slightly increasing your upwards reach.
Obtained at Level 2.

### Dash
* Input: DASH TAP or HOLD (Keybind)
* Increases acceleration and grants bonus Momentum for up to 2 seconds. Jumping off a ledge while dashing will increase jump distance and speed. Performing a precise landing while Dash is on cooldown instantly resets it.
* How to get it: Challenge: Dash Training Located northeast of the Riser time trial, towards the edge of Downtown.

### Powerslide
* Input: DESCEND Hold (Preserves Chain)
* Boosts speed momentarily. Powersliding towards a low obstacle will cause you to slide underneath it.
* How to get it: Challenge: The Art of Powerslide South of the building where Solar time trial is found

### Dropdown
* Input: DESCEND (Preserves Chain)
* Powersliding towards a ledge will activate Dropdown. Slightly reduces fall damage, increasing maximum fall distance. Powersliding off a ledge at high speeds will not activate Dropdown.
* How to get it: Challenge: Dropper North of Apollo

### Afterboost
* Input: DASH Hold to charge ➜ RELEASE ➜ DASH TAP
* Afterboost allows you to access long jump, but can be useful on its own. To activate Afterboost, charge your dash until it's full. When you release your dash, your dash bar will turn red. While it's red, if you press dash down again, you will get a minor velocity boost, and activate Afterboost for a short moment. While Afterboost is active, jumping off a ledge will cause you to long jump. Activating Afterboost gives you a boost in momentum, represented by the orange section of your momentum bar. This bonus momentum will persist until you touch the ground again.
* How to get it: Challenge: A Longer Jump South of The Art of Powerslide starting point

### Wall Boost
* Input: [ AIR DASH ] ➜ ASCEND x2 DOUBLE TAP
* Holding Dash midair will activate "air dash". While air dashing, look upwards and double-tap Ascend against a wall to activate Wall Boost. Grants significant upwards velocity, allowing for greater speed and efficiency than a regular wall climb.
* How to get it: Challenge: Wall Climb Boost Southwest from Advancement Beta

### Wall Kick
* Input: [ AIR DASH ] ➜ ASCEND (Back against an object + Midair)
* Holding Dash midair will activate "air dash". While air dashing, face your back to a wall and tap Ascend. Kicks off the wall, redirecting you towards the direction you are looking.
* How to get it: Challenge: Leg Day On the roof of Nimbus

### Chain
* Input: ASCEND TAP or HOLD
* Long Jump to begin a chain, indicated by the red outline around your momentum bar. While a chain is active, landing and quickly jumping off another ledge will cause you to Long Jump once more, continuing the chain. Speed and distance increases with each subsequent chain jump. Moves like powerslide and dropdown preserve your chain.
* How to get it: At the top of Vertigo

## Combat

### Dropkick
* Input: [ COIL ] ➜ ATTACK (Midair)
* Coil and press <Attack> to dropkick. Knocks players back based on your velocity. Dropkick stops upwards movement, and slightly redirects if forward. It can also be used while swinging to redirect your trajectory. Landing while dropkicking briefly immobilizes you, but you can Powerslide out of it.
Obtained at Level 5.

## Other

### Combo
* Input: View Full Page
* A combo will begin automatically as you parkour. Going to areas you have not yet been during the combo will extend your combo's "coverage". The faster you extend your coverage, the higher your score will be. You will be granted XP at the end of a combo based on your total score.
Obtained at Level 10.

## Basic

### High Jump
* Input: ASCEND HOLD
* High Jump is pretty simple, holding down jump allows you to jump higher by altering your gravity.

### Vault
* Input: ASCEND TAP or HOLD
* Vaulting an object is a quick way to smoothly pass over it. While vaulting, you can hold jump for a powerful vault jump. If you release your movement input while holding space, you will springboard instead.

### Mantle
* Input: ASCEND
* To mantle a ledge instead of normally climbing it, your head must be over the edge. Mantling allows you to springboard, and is faster than a normal ledge climb.

### Springboard
* Input: ASCEND HOLD (Release other movement keys)
* Springboard is a powerful vertical jump, and can be done out of either a mantle or a vault. To springboard, release your movement input and hold space during a mantle or a vault.

### Wall Climb
* Input: ASCEND HOLD
* The Wall Climb is an essential movement for vertical traversal. By default, you can wall climb only once until you touch the ground again. If you're falling too fast, you will not be able to grip the wall to climb it. The height you climb depends on your Momentum. Pressing DESCEND during a wall climb stops it. The reticle turns gray when you begin to fall, indicating you can't wall climb.

### Wall Run
* Input: FORWARD / BACKWARD + ASCEND HOLD
* The Wall Run is an essential movement for horizontal traversal. By default, you can wall run only twice until you touch the ground again. To use the second wall run, you need to wall run on a surface with a different angle from the last. The speed you run along the wall depends on your Momentum. By default, releasing the jump button allows you to jump out of the wall run, and will redirect your velocity forwards. Tapping DESCEND during a wall run will cut it short. Your wall run ammo will start flashing when it's about to run out.

### Wall Jump
* Input: ASCEND
* Wall Jump is a blanket term used to refer to any jump coming out of a wall climb or wall run. Wall run jumps are generally considered more powerful than wall climb jumps, however both have their use cases. Visit the page for Wall Jump or Wall Run for more specific information.

### Slide Not To Be Confused With Powerslide
* Input: DESCEND Hold
* A slide happens when you hold DESCEND on a slope, while under an object, or walk on a very steep slope.You can also perform a slide by landing while coiling.

### Air Dash
* Input: DASH TAP or HOLD (Midair)
* Air dashing is indicated by a purple bar above the Momentum bar, and will appear when the DASH button is held while airborne.

### Climb
* Input: FORWARDS or BACKWARDS
* When clutching onto a climbable object, you can climb upwards by holding [ FORWARDS ] and downwards by holding [ BACKWARDS ] .

### Climb (Leap)
* Input: ASCEND
* While climbing, you can leap upwards using ASCEND . There's a short cooldown before you can perform it again.

### Climb (Jump)
* Input: ASCEND
* While climbing, you can jump off using ASCEND . If you are facing towards the object you are climbing, you will instead do a [ CLIMB (LEAP) ] .

### Flail Jump
* Input: DASH ➜ ASCEND (At an edge)
* A flail jump is performed similarly to a long jump: by holding DASH and pressing ASCEND at an edge. The distance gained is further than a normal jump, but not as far as a long jump.

### Impact
* Input: DESCEND (Before landing)
* When you are about to land, press DESCEND to reduce fall damage. Depending on whether or not you were moving, the impact animation will change.

### Fall Stabilization
* Input: DESCEND HOLD + ASCEND HOLD (Midair)
* While falling, you can hold down ASCEND and DESCEND to prevent spinning out of control.

## Intermediate

### Long Jump
* Input: [ AFTERBOOST ] ➜ ASCEND (At an edge)
* Long Jump is a very powerful horizontal jump. To Long Jump, activate afterboost and quickly jump off a ledge. You can combine this ability with High Jump for even more distance. Your velocity will become "committed" as it goes past your Momentum, causing you to lose air control unless explicitly altered by movement inputs. Meaning, look before you leap!

### Wall Clutch
* Input: [ AIR DASH ] ➜ ASCEND
* Wall Clutch allows you to suspend yourself on a wall for a short period of time. Unlike a wall climb, this can be performed while falling. To perform it, hold DASH midair to activate [ AIR DASH ] . At this point, pressing ASCEND on a wall will activate wall clutch instead of wall climbing. This movement is what enables wall boosting.

### Wall Bounce
* Input: ASCEND TAP
* When facing a wall, tapping ASCEND midair and without pressing any movement keys will cause you to bounce backwards. Quickly flicking in a direction allows you to redirect your velocity towards it. You can also perform it by quickly exiting a wall climb (though the timing is tight).

## Advanced

### Coyote Time
* Input: [ PASSIVE ]
* Coyote Time is a passive ability which essentially makes you act like you're on ground when you're actually midair. It's triggered whenever you leave the ground without pressing ASCEND . Jumping during coyote time counts as you being on an edge, meaning afterboost will result in a long jump regardless of when you entered coyote time.

### Jump Boost
* Input: [ COYOTE TIME ] ➜ [ WALL BOOST ] ➜ [ HIGH JUMP ] OR ASCEND
* A Jump Boost is when you jump along the edge of an object with significant upwards velocity, compounding your jump force with the velocity you already have.

## Other

### Ledge Grab
* Input: ASCEND While ascending or after a Pipe Climb
* You clump your hands together to grab onto the ledge above you and raise your entire body onto it.

### Wide Ledge Grab
* Input: ASCEND While falling or after a Wall Climb
* You extend your hands to your shoulders and grab onto the ledge above you, raising your entire body onto it.

### Barrier Vault
* Input: ASCEND While near or climbing onto a thin object
* You grab onto the thin object's top side which you are climbing on and raise your body in a vault-like motion over it.

## Removed

### Slide Boost
* Input: [ SLIDE ] ➜ ASCEND TAP or HOLD
* Slide boosting was performed by sliding after falling from a short height then jumping out of it within a small time frame. The boost in velocity you get depends on how far you fell before initiating it.

## Notes

* Additive Jump Velocity is the term used for when a jump's velocity gets added on to by another move.

* Try experimenting with techniques yourself; most, but not all variants may be showcased on this page.
