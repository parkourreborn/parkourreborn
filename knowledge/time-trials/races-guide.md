Races are short courses spread across the city: time trials and challenges. There are also PvP races against other players.

## Time Trials

Every time trial has bronze, silver, gold and platinum medal times. Bronze gives 1 rank star, silver 2, gold 3 and platinum 4 (a full rank up), counting every lower medal. Difficulties from easiest: basic, moderate, tough, advanced, insane. Players can teleport to time trials they have interacted with.

## Challenges

To complete a challenge you reach its end goal through a series of checkpoints within a time limit. Rewards are rank stars (some challenges teach an ability, advancement challenges unlock the next 10 ranks). Completed challenges can be warped to from the map.

### Downtown Challenges

* **Dash Training**: Teaches Dash. Northeast from Riser. Difficulty: Basic; Checkpoints: 3; Time limit: 19.000 seconds; Reward: 2 rank stars.
* **The Art of Powerslide**: Teaches Powerslide. West from Solar. Difficulty: Basic; Checkpoints: 3; Time limit: 15.000 seconds; Reward: 2 rank stars.
* **Dropper**: Teaches Dropdown. North from Apollo. Difficulty: Moderate; Checkpoints: 5; Time limit: 18.000 seconds; Reward: 2 rank stars.
* **A Longer Jump**: Teaches Long Jump. West from The Art of Powerslide. Difficulty: Moderate; Checkpoints: 5; Time limit: 30.000 seconds; Reward: 3 rank stars; Rank Required: 2.
* **Advancement Alpha** (advancement challenge): Northeast from Apollo. Difficulty: Tough; Checkpoints: 4; Time limit: 65.000 seconds; Reward: 10 rank stars; Rank Cap: +10.

### Dirwik Challenges

* **Wall Climb Boost**: Teaches Wall Boost. Southwest from Advancement Beta. Difficulty: Tough; Checkpoints: 6; Time limit: 53.000 seconds; Reward: 5 rank stars; Rank Required: 3; Restriction: Wall Climb disabled.
* **Leg Day**: Teaches Wall Kick. On the roof of Nimbus. Difficulty: Advanced; Checkpoints: 2; Time limit: 15.000 seconds; Reward: 5 rank stars; Rank Required: 3.
* **Advancement Beta** (advancement challenge): West of GL Tower. Difficulty: Insane; Checkpoints: 9; Time limit: 104.000 seconds; Reward: 10 rank stars; Rank Required: 10; Rank Cap: +10.

### Stack Challenges

* **Advancement Gamma** (advancement challenge): At the Drop Off checkpoint, behind Zephyr. Difficulty: Insane; Checkpoints: 11; Time limit: 134.000 seconds; Reward: 10 rank stars; Rank Required: 20; Rank Cap: +10.

## PvP Races

PvP Races involve competing with other players and getting to the end goal (randomly selected nearby points) before they do.

* To invite someone to a race, click on them in the player list, then click "Challenge." To invite more players, do the same thing.

* Once all participants have gathered around the creator of the race, the host may press ‘START.’

* Once the first participant reaches the end, all other participants have 60 seconds to complete the race before they are disqualified.

* Once all participants have completed the race, or if the 60 second timer runs out, a summary is displayed, and players may rematch if they wish.

* A race's finish is usually determined by whichever endpoint is closest to 150 meters away from the race leader.

## Trivia

* There can only be a certain number of time trials per district depending on its size, most of them having a maximum of 6.

* This ensures the rank stars are spread out evenly, considering that players will eventually be able to reach rank 100.

* Vestibule's time trial name was temporarily changed to Vegetable for April Fools (2025).

* Glass has had it's ending point changed 3 times.

* In alpha 1.72, a UI was added to show how many attempts you have taken on a time trial or challenge.
