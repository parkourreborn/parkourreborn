## Lore

While still rudimentary, lore does exist within the world of Reborn.

### Runners

Players are known as "Runners," people who illegally parkour across the rooftops of Verna.

* Runners mainly complete jobs such as missions.

* The Runner's end-goal is to take down the CPD and stop their wrongdoings.

### Civil Protections Department

The Civil Protections Department, or CPD for short, is an organization set out to capture Runners and stop them from messing with the city's infrastructure.

* They are the main antagonist of Runners and the story as a whole.

* They're essentially a corrupt government of sorts.

* They are likely inspired by the CPF from Mirror's Edge, who fulfill a similar role.

* The CPD might send out guards around the city to stop any passing Runners.

### Verna

* Verna is an authoritarian, oligarchy style government.

* Most citizens are undocumented and not part of the government's authorities.

### Bomb Fruit

An odd species of fruit of unknown origin that look ridiculous, grow upside down, and smell like slightly expired milk; their existence is solely responsible for the fabrication of the most powerful bombs ever made by mankind. Somehow edible.

## Difficulty

At the start of the game, the player is presented with two difficulty options: Casual and Traceur. They're only keybind/setting presets, and don't actually alter the "difficulty" of the game. To switch between the two, players can manually change the corresponding keybinds and settings through the tab menu.

"Casual gives you more standard keybinds and makes parkour less input-intensive. Traceur gives you keybinds more akin to what a Legacy player may find familiar."

### Casual

"Less inputs required, but takes away some control from the player

Recommended if new to gaming"

* Auto Parkour (setting): ON

* Descend (keybind): CTRL

* Dash (keybind): SHIFT

### Traceur

"More inputs required, but gives full control to the player

Recommended if experienced with gaming"

* Auto Parkour (setting): OFF

* Descend (keybind): SHIFT

* Dash (keybind): MMB aka Middle Mouse Button / Scroll Wheel

## Movement Details

Main article: Movement

Movement is the main gameplay focus of Reborn, and what makes it a parkour game.

* Movement abilities are obtained mostly by completing challenges scattered throughout the city.

* Some abilities are found via books, such as coil, chain, and dropkick.

* Leveling up increases your momentum cap (up to Level 20).

* When falling at high speeds, you will spin out of control. This can be stabilized by holding down ASCEND and DESCEND at the same time.

* Wall running has a limit (also known as a cap) to how far down you can go on a wall with it, called "fall distance."

## Respawning

Main article: Checkpoints

A Checkpoint

* Checkpoints are objects which players can sync to. While synced to a checkpoint, you will respawn there until you sync to a new one or enter an activated spawn region's radius.

* The button required to sync to a checkpoint is ALT ACTION .

* Syncing to a checkpoint that you haven't synced to before gives you some XP. Undiscovered checkpoints blink green instead of blue, and are much brighter.

* Using the warp core premium upgrade, players can instantly teleport to discovered checkpoints anywhere on the map.

* Respawn time decreases while falling, which is why resetting manually through the Roblox menu with low velocity takes longer.

* If you get stuck, you can teleport to Main Spawn by clicking the 'Return to Spawn' button at the bottom of settings, then waiting for a 10 second timer to pass (300 seconds at the time).

* The signal booster premium upgrade allows you to respawn anywhere infinitely.

## Gear

Main article: Gear

Gear are items held in the inventory that affect how players move around and interact with the world.

* Many are upgradeable. Most have two separate paths, each with two or three levels. You can only upgrade a gear into one of its paths; to use the other, you must acquire another copy to upgrade.

* Gear can be crafted with resources found across the map and/or bought from vendors. Some gear are bought from vendors directly.

* There are four different types, each equipped in different slots:

* Core: Equivalent to right arm gear from Legacy, and are the "core" of your playstyle.

* Augment: Equivalent to left arm gear from Legacy, and "augment" your playstyle with significant movement abilities.

* Compound: Equivalent to back gear from Legacy, and are a less powerful kind of augment gear that is more gimmicky.

* Utility: Equivalent to hip gear from Legacy, and serve as a more basic general-usage gear to give your playstyle an extra touch or provide conveniences. Two utility gear can be equipped simultaneously, in both a left and right slot.

## NPCs

Main article: NPCs

### Vendors

 Vendors are NPCs that sell various items.

* Some items are obtained by finding Vendors and buying them, instead of being found somewhere in the city.

* Certain Vendors will move around, so you'll need to check different spots to find them again.

* Unlike Legacy, most gear is not locked behind a level requirement. However, some NPCs will refuse to sell you items if you are below a specific rank.

* Every Vendor sells common items, like consumables and resources. You can buy as many common items as you want at the same price, but the price for gear gets more expensive with each purchase.

* Some items—even if they're common—will be in limited stock, but the price rule still applies to them.

* Most Vendors have a unique gear/item or upgrade(s) that can't be found elsewhere.

## Races

Main article: Races

### Time Trials

Time trials are short courses that require players to go from point A to B within a set time. Each medal has a different material type, and are awarded based on how quickly a time trial is completed, which in turn gives varying amounts of rank stars.

* All attainable medals, from lowest to highest material type, are: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum.

* Most districts will have up to 6 time trials.

* The total rank stars earned from a medal includes all preceding medals.

* This means that a platinum medal will reward a player with a full rank up.

* In actuality, the decimal count for a trial's time is 8 digits, but only 3 are visually shown in the details menu when standing nearby.

## Rank & Level

Rank and Level are aspects of skill, with each representing a different part of gameplay.

### Rank

Rank 31, with 9 Rank Stars

Ranks indicate how skilled a player is at parkour.

A player will rank up when obtaining over 10 rank stars. You can obtain them via:

* Races

* Time trial medals (240)

* Specific challenges (49)

* Currently, all of them reward rank stars.

* Completing certain missions (5)

* Entering new districts (25)

* 10 from Downtown, 5 from the rest.

The total amount obtainable from each method as of now is contained within parenthesis next to them.

There will be more ways to obtain them in later updates. Once every 10 Rank Ups, you must complete an 'Advancement Challenge' to "prove" your skill for the next line of Ranks. The highest attainable rank as of now is 31, with 9 excess stars.

### Level

Main article: Multipliers

Level 111, with 95.1% completion and an x8.40 multiplier

Levels indicate how much a player has explored the map.

A player will level up when accumulating over 100% of the experience points (XP for short) required. You can accumulate them via:

* Syncing to undiscovered checkpoints

* Obtaining time trial medals for the first time

* Completing certain missions

* Achieving combos

There will be more ways to accumulate them in later updates. There is no cap to level, meaning you can theoretically attain an infinite amount.

## Visuals & Interface

* Moving at high velocities will display speed lines around your camera, alongside the camera beginning to shake intensely. The camera shaking intensity can be adjusted in settings under the 'CAMERA' category.

* Idle animations occur automatically after standing still for a few seconds (you can activate a random one manually with G ).

* Holding down K on PC for 5 seconds and then releasing will reset all of your menu keybinds.

* You can spectate players by clicking their name with the list in the upper right corner.

### Day/Night Cycle

In real-world time.

* Day lasts for 12 minutes.

* Night lasts for 10 minutes.

* A full cycle lasts for 22 minutes.

## Currency

Main article: Currency

* There are 2 types of currency: standard ( Credits), and premium ( Tokens).

* Tokens can be bought through the purchase tokens pop-up menu for Robux, while Credits can be obtained through various means, such as completing missions, selling valuables, and more.

* All items can be bought with either currency, but upgrades and gear will be more expensive to buy with Tokens.

## Commands

Use /e before the command to hide it from chat

### Removed

* %bye - Kicked you from the game without saving.

* %spawn - Sent you to Main Spawn after a 10 second timer passed.

* Has since been replaced by a debug button under settings at the very bottom of it.

* %tutorial - Brought you to the start of the Tutorial in the tutorial walkthrough state.

* %unstuck - Same as %tutorial.

* %join - Made you rejoin the server you were currently in.

* %join <username> - Teleported you to the server the specified user was in as long as they were your friend.

* %fix OR %f - Refreshed your character state as an attempt to fix any related bugs.

## Resetting Data

Players can reset their data by going into settings, scrolling to the bottom, and clicking whichever reset button is applicable for them. Resetting data is irreversible.

* RESET PLAYER DATA DESCRIPTION: ARE YOU SURE? Resetting your data is PERMANENT and IRREVERSIBLE! Everything will be reset! The only thing that will not reset is your playtime. If you have purchased Tokens, you will get them back if you've spent them. Once you reset your data, you will need to rejoin the game. To prevent abuse, you may only reset your data once per week.

* Resetting your data is PERMANENT and IRREVERSIBLE! If you are sure, please type in your username below. You will need to rejoin afterwards!

* RESET TIME TRIALS DESCRIPTION: Resetting your trial times is PERMANENT and IRREVERSIBLE! This also means your rank will be lowered accordingly! Challenges, however, will not be wiped. Once you reset your trial times, you will need to rejoin the game.

* ARE YOU REALLY SURE? CANCEL Resetting your data is PERMANENT and IRREVERSIBLE! If you are sure, please type in your username below. You will need to rejoin afterwards!

* RESET SETTINGS DESCRIPTION: Are you a CASUAL or TRACEUR player? This will override what you chose at the start of the game. Casual gives you more standard keybinds. Traceur gives you keybinds more akin to what a PARKOUR Legacy player may find familiar. Choosing either option will also reset all of your settings to their default values, ensuring your controls and preferences match the selected playstyle exactly.
