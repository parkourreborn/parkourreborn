Alpha 1.62 was released on October 29, 2025 (build 202). It was the Halloween 2025 seasonal update.

## Tweaks And Bug Fixes
* Archived old Tower badges and replaced them with new ones because they were incorrectly given out before
* Fixed Tower badges not being awarded correctly
* Fixed Gear badges not being awarded retroactively
* Fixed Advancement badges not being awarded retroactively
* Fixed another error related to infinite loading if you had deleted missions
* Made chat announcements use display name instead of username
* Fixed navigator not updating when not searching anything and discovering new map pins
* Made map automatically update pins when discovering new checkpoints
