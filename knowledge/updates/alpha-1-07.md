Alpha 1.07 was released on December 16, 2023 (build 82).

## Changes

* Revamped compass
* Time trials, their end-points, and party members now appear on the compass
* Revamped grappler crosshair
* Lag introduced in 1.06 is now resolved

* Fixed moving objects flinging you into the 5th dimension
* Fixed infinite climbing

(we're working on vertigo don't mind it)
