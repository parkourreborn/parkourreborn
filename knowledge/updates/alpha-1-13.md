Alpha 1.13 was released on March 7, 2024 (build 116).

## Changes

* New "Loadout" window
* You can now equip gear by dragging and dropping them into the loadout window
* You can now inspect other players' loadouts by clicking on them on the playerlist
* Hovering over a gear now shows its level without needing to click on it

* New original music track from Downtown Binary!
* Added a short buffer before starting a wall climb/run after a springboard/vault jump when using auto parkour
* Brightened crafting hex grid background
* Optimized playerlist performance
* Optimized item display/rotation performance
