Pre-Alpha 1.4 was released on November 20, 2023 (build 57).

## Changes

* **Dirwik has been unlocked for exploration!**

* Updated several areas in Downtown

* Fixed ranks
* Added setting to disable window tweens when transitioning between day/night, improves performance for some people
* Updated wide ledge climb animation
* Split "Visuals" settings category into "Graphics" and "Interface"
* Various bug fixes
* Internal optimizations
