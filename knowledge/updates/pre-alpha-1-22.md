Pre-Alpha 1.22 was released on November 8, 2023 (build 52).

## Changes

* New %join chat command! Use it to join your friends (and only your friends) currently playing PARKOUR Reborn!
* Respawn regions! Climb some tall stuff and see if you can find the terminals!
* Updated Vertigo interior

* Shift Lock is now rebindable
* Various audio-related updates, new pad landing sound
