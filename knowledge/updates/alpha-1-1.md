Alpha 1.1 was released on February 9, 2024 (build 94).

## Changes

Reborn is now**FREE TO PLAY** until Monday!

* Yank Grappler is now craftable
* Glove is now craftable

* Missions are now functional! New players will get "tutorial" missions as they level

* Major Vertigo interior rework
  * We are actively working on improving and expanding it, give us feedback!

* Added respawn "lives"
  * When respawning using a tranceiver your signal strength will dwindle, refreshing upon syncing with a checkpoint. The amount of strength impacted per respawn is determined by how many checkpoints you're missing in the tranceiver's respective district.

* "Now Playing" now pops up when a song begins playing
* Current track is displayed at the top of the tab menu
* 3 brand new original music tracks!
  * Currently, only "Searching" and "Letting Go" are adaptive to your momentum. We are also slowly phasing out the old music from Downtown Binary's "Aurora" album for licensing reasons.

* New resource, "Rugged Synthetics"
* New resource, "Work Gloves"
* New resource type, gear mods
* Changed beginning of tutorial slightly
* Mantle now smoother
* Barrier vault (fences etc.) now smoother
* Improved grappler targeting of ziplines
* Grappler crosshair flips when targeting a swing-able object
* Transition to wall climb/run now smoother
* All new custom character physics (let us know if you encounter any issues!)
* New non-constraint, scripted swinging physics
* Performance improvements probably
* New running animations
* New backwards hard land animation
* New climb animation
* Various movement polish and QoL improvements
{{Hotfix|date=February 10, 2024}}
* Fixed missions not working if you rejoin!
* Attempting to address the loading issues!
