Alpha 1.541 was released on September 20, 2025 (build 192).

## Tweaks & Bug Fixes
* Made it possible to jump out of Mag Rail’s pipe climb ability
* Added a toggle for item VFX
* Improved Connor’s idle animation
* Fixed infinite loading caused by old missions, trials, or challenges being improperly handled when deleted by us
* Fixed infinite loading if you use the Microsoft Store Roblox client
* Fixed race invites not working sometimes
* Fixed a potential issue related to Token gifting
* Removed %time commands from Content Creators
* Removed %servermessage from Content Creators
* Fixed a few infinite fall locations
* Fixed a specific pipe in between Dirwik and Downtown being wiped
