Pre-Alpha 1.3 was released on November 16, 2023 (build 53).

## Changes

* **PARTIES**
* Parties have finally arrived!

Party invites can be sent by clicking someones name in the player list on the top right. You can also kick players as the party leader by clicking on them, and you can leave a party by clicking on yourself.

* Fixed most player list bugs
* Hovering over the player list now displays all players in the server
* New carousel setting type for parties

Coming in part 2
* Party members on compass
* Spectating
* Player loadout inspection
* Player net profiles
