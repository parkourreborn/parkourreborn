Alpha 1.63 was released on November 1, 2025 (build 205).

## Goodbye, Halloween!
We hope that you enjoyed the event while it lasted!
* Disabled Halloween features
* Reverted map, props, and NPC outfits back to defaults

## Tweaks And Bug Fixes
* Made store button prettier
* Hid the compass UI while in the title screen
* Fixed being unable to pick up valuables in snow
* Fixed steep slope ascension
* Tweaked some areas in Downtown and Dirwik to be more beginner-friendly
