Pre-Alpha 1.21 was released on November 7, 2023 (build 48).

## Changes

* All pre-alpha players will get a badge next to their name on the player list (rejoin if you don't see yours!)

* Hovering over players on the player list shows their rank and level
* Player list badges now have tooltips upon hovering
* Player list position bumps out with the menu
* Minor miscellaneous adjustments to player list
* Fixed leaving players lingering on the player list
* Fixed "Ocean bug"
* Fixed "Freeze dash"
