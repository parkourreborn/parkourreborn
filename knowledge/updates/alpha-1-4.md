Alpha 1.4 was released on December 24, 2024 (build 159).

## Artemis
Artemis, the tallest building in Dirwik, has gotten a complete rework - featuring an all-new interior climbing challenge in order to reach the top and activate the transceiver.

## New Gear Upgrades
* Added several new crafting materials to be discovered
* Swing Grappler II &Yank Grappler II are now craftable!
* Climber Glove I &Drifter Glove I are now craftable!

## Pvp Races
Due to an internal error, this feature is currently unavailable. We will be hotfixing this shortly.

## New Music
  * SIX** new original music tracks have been added to to the default playlist!
* Aerial by Downtown Binary
* Echo by Downtown Binary
* Float by Downtown Binary
* Points by Downtown Binary
* Rise by Downtown Binary
* Signals by Downtown Binary

* Added extra info to gear when selecting them in your inventory that describes their effects
* A message will be displayed in chat announcing when a player scores more than 100,000 in a combo or every 100 chain jumps in a row a player lands
* Added a notification icon on the mission button for new missions (we will be polishing its behavior soon, deliveries currently falsely trigger it)
* Modified some component spawn locations
* Several more graffitis have been added to the map
* Updated credits

Players who previously had "Grappler Mechanisms" will now see this item turn into "Nanofiber Cables". Don't worry, this is technically an upgrade!
