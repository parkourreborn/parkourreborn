Alpha 1.41 was released on February 14, 2025 (build 176).

## Changes

* **All major memory leaks have been fixed!!**
## Pvp Races
  * **Race against others to randomly selected nearby points!**
* To invite someone to a race, click on them in the player list, then click "Challenge." To invite more players, do the same thing
* Once all participants have gathered around the creator of the race, the host may press ‘START.’
* Once the first participant reaches the end, all other participants have 60 seconds to complete the race before they are disqualified
* Once all participants have completed the race, or if the 60 second timer runs out, a summary is displayed, and players may rematch if they wish

## Map View
(replaces 'FAST TRAVEL' button)
  * **Get a birds-eye view of the map, conveniently regain your bearings, navigate activities, place markers, and more!**
* By default, press M to open the map, or use the button in the menu
* For now, all time trials will be able to be fast travelled to by anyone

## Daily Rewards
  * **Every 24 hours—if you’ve entered the game at least once during the day—a reward is left pending for you to claim! Its menu button can be found in the top left.**
* On the second week, rewards are increased slightly from the first; in the future, rewards will amp up further than this
* The claim button works as a total pool, meaning all past and current pending rewards get claimed at once

## Vip Gamepass
  * **Purchase the new “Executive Runner” gamepass for R$299 to enjoy DOUBLE credits & XP!**

## Premium Store & Gear Purchasing
Unfortunately, due to internal complications this feature has been postponed to a future update.

## Movement Changes
* Wall running into the ground now cancels the wall run after 100ms instead of instantly/never
* Chain is no longer held indefinitely during coyote time while moving upwards
* Bhopping has been re-added/fixed; now, when you land, you get 100ms of air-friction instead of ground-friction, which allows you to maintain your velocity more easily by repeatedly jumping

## World Improvements
  * **Tons of general improvements to already existing districts, including: continuity, visuals, and more!**
* Changes to the Tutorial to better explain landing/rolling to new players
* Added 1-way exit elevators near the top of challenge towers, as well as after the first Artemis checkpoint
* Some previously barren areas are now detailed
* Reworked multiple interiors and exteriors
* Several more graffiti, many new loiter spots, and new props have been added
* Lots of little easter eggs scattered around—good luck finding them all!

## Bug Fixes
  * **All major memory leaks have been fixed!!**
* Added the missing third “Nanotube Cable” onto the map
* Crafting on console has hopefully been fixed this time lol
* Fixed title screen camera behaving weirdly while loading in
* Fixed new tutorial sometimes breaking for new players
* Joining the game should no longer have you spawn outside the map
* Buildings highlighting after exiting a race while in a party has been fixed
* Slowing down on pipes/ladders when clutching onto them from a large height works again
* A couple of map-related issues relating to certain textures and parts being misplaced have been fixed

## Miscellaneous
* Updated the credits list in the main menu to include all new members of the studio
* Your chain counter gets automatically reset back to 0 if you chain multiple times too quickly, such as backwards on a slope; this is to prevent mass farming of chains and stop chats from getting flooded
* Added setting for fake first-person shadows–disable if you see gray shapes in first person
* Health and username gets displayed above players now upon mouse hover
* The "Wall Climb Boost" challenge has received various quality-of-life improvements
* Additional button added to the top left to access the premium store menu
* Reduced overall volume of air conditioning units
* New running and dashing animations
* Added wind volume slider in settings
* Fine-tuned button sizes in the menu
