Alpha 1.42 was released on February 26, 2025 (build 179).

## Guide Arrows
Most challenges now feature arrows which direct you along the standard route!

## Bug Fixes
Most major memory leaks have been fixed!

* Fixed a couple of freefall spots and 2 unrendered places on the map
* Fixed map view's camera position breaking if you used the Binoculars before opening it
* Fixed resetting while in a time trial disabling fall damage
* Fixed map view being locked after doing a PvP race
* Fixed being able to use the Binoculars during loading and in the title screen
* Fixed the Zipline Hook’s model disappearing in first-person if you zoomed out and back in while riding a zipline

## Miscellaneous
* Content Creators are distinguished by a camera badge instead of using Moderator's shield one
* Some additional optimizations have been made that should hopefully reduce stuttering and increase FPS
* A new interior has been added in Fragment among other minor map changes, notably in south Downtown & south Dirwik
* Several new drones have been placed around Downtown, Dirwik, & Fragment

{{Hotfix|date=February 28, 2025}}
* A small patch to fix new players not being able to select an option in the title screen as well as to fix upgrades in the shop not being purchasable.
