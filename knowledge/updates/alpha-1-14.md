Alpha 1.14 was released on April 10, 2024 (build 117).

## Changes

* **Improved new-player experience**
* "Open menu" tutorial for new players
* Many tutorial improvements
* Tutorial text write-out tech improvements
* Casual/Traceur selection screen improvements

  * **Tons of new quality-of-life features**
* Grappler now plays a short sound when its charges reload
* Grappler crosshair's size now depends on number of charges available
* Releasing spacebar while jumping now immediately puts you in the falling state, this improves responsiveness when you're jumping just barely over a floor by immediately grounding you to it when you release space
* Improved dash bar
* Dash now automatically activates the moment it cools down if you press and hold the dash button during the cooldown
* Doors can now be opened with Interact or Dash
* "Return to spawn" debug button in settings
* "Exit to hub" button in place of menu button while menu is open
* Added hotkeys for Inventory, Missions, Loadout, and The Net
* You can now track as many missions as you want
* Improved mission trackers
* 3 ambience tracks that dynamically adjust themselves based on your environment
* New hard landing & very hard landing sounds
* "Mute on focus lost" option now has adjustable unfocused volume when disabled
* Improved teleport and loading screen

  * **Several fixes to long-standing annoyances**
* Fixed party highlights (world highlighting still exists i think, not sure how it even happens)
* Fixed party inviting and playerlist weirdness
* Fixed inconsistencies detecting whether or not you can climb on top of a pipe
* Fixed player limb collisions randomly enabling themselves
* Fixed issue with auto parkour not working when wall/pipe jumping
* Fixed prompts flickering
* Fixed wall bounce improperly transferring velocity
* Bottom right HUD section now comes out with the menu rightbar
* Slightly optimized menu
{{Hotfix|date=April 14, 2024}}
* Fixed chat
* Fixed player freezing until you open the menu, hint now displays correctly
