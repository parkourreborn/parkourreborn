Alpha 1.0 was released on November 23, 2023 (build 60).

## Gear
* Gear is now acquirable, through**crafting**!

## Crafting
* Craft gear by finding their resources, and combining them in your inventory!

* Resources have unique spawn points throughout the map, and can only be collected once.
* Core resources are similar, but there will only be**one** place to find each one

* Inventory is now properly sorted and split into categories

* Fixed stumble landing counting towards landings stat
* Sped up loading a bit
