Alpha 1.11 was released on February 12, 2024 (build 100).

## Changes

Sorry about the physics issues! We should have left them in the oven for just a little longer!
As an apology and a thank you for sticking around, we've extended the free period to Tuesday @ 7pm EST

* Reverted physics, most problems should be resolved!
* Reverted swing physics

* Added new missions
* Fixed "Discover Checkpoints" mission, in theory it should now adjust accordingly if you've discovered all or almost all checkpoints on the map
* Changed third-person auto rotate behavior a little
* Slightly nerfed/changed slide boost (not a bug)
* Players who have paid for access will now properly get their feather
* Changed rank star appearance slightly
* Changed dropdown behavior
* Fixed glass not breaking after the first try
* Many more bug fixes
