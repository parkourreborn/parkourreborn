Alpha 1.51 was released on August 10, 2025 (build 187).

## Purchasable Gear
You are now able to purchase gear in the Premium Store using Tokens!

## Npc Shop Rebalance
The token cost of crafting items have been rebalanced to make more sense.

## Harper Adjustments
Harper’s minimum rank has been changed from Rank 10 to Rank 8!

## Chat Text Colors
Developers, staff members, playtesters, and executive runners now have unique message colors in the chat!

## Time Trial Adjustments
These time trials have received difficulty adjustments:
* Riser: Tough -> Moderate
* Glass: Tough -> Moderate
* Genesis: Moderate -> Tough
* Vestibule: Advanced -> Tough
* Solar: Moderate -> Advanced
* Sawdust: Advanced -> Tough
* Celsius: Moderate -> Advanced
* Flow: Tough -> Advanced (Platinum medal time increased from 16 to 16.5)
* Ascension: Advanced -> Moderate

## Map Adjustments
Dirwik has received new checkpoints and minor map changes to help flow for newer players! Downtown and Highrise also received minor map fixes!

## Bug Fixes And Tweaks
* Moved one pulse generator
* Removed cooldown on Mag Rail wallrun launch
* XP Router added on top of Highrise
* Fixed being able to spectate while not grounded
* You can now open the chat from a closed state by pressing the / key
* Being able to dropkick stack while frozen has been patched
* Being unable to open your map sometimes has been fixed

## Free Period Extended
Free period extended to August 15, 2025 at 12:00 AM
