Alpha 1.12 was released on February 24, 2024 (build 106).

## Changes

Inventories have been reset out of necessity, we apologize for the inconvenience.
* New original track by Downtown Binary!
* Added a menu for changing music

* Added setting to enable/disable camera jump tilt
* Dash now boosts your momentum acceleration up to a point
* Tokens can now be purchased, however they do not currently serve any purpose

* Fixed "now playing" being spammed
* Fixed gravity breaking when spamming jump while respawning
* Fixed repeatedly sliding on slopes incrementally stacking velocity
* Fixed lingering tooltips
* Fixed unable to grab on to ziplines
* Fixed being able to exit available map
* Internal optimizations
* Various bugfixes
* Various polish
{{Hotfix|date=February 24, 2024}}
* %Spawn is back
