Alpha 1.71 was released on December 23, 2025 (build 224). It was the Christmas 2025 seasonal update.

## Movement Tweaks
Sliding finally got some love! To be clear, we are not talking about powerslide!
* Regular slide now properly sticks you to slopes
* Sliding now has some friction (and won't last forever)

## Cosmetic Fixes
Users who lost bags in a previous version of the game should have them automatically given back to you, check your inventory!
Users living in regions where paid random items are restricted have had all of their cosmetics refunded. Why? There was an issue where the promised cosmetic was not received properly. We are sincerely sorry for this inconvenience.
* Restricted region users now get the cosmetic they are promised
* Restricted region users can no longer change the assigned gear of a bag after revealing the contents
* Patched several currency/cosmetic duplication glitches
* Fixed not being charged the correct amount when buying multiple bags with an assigned gear
* Cable Glider tab in cosmetic inventory made more visible - use your scroll wheel!
* Fixed Legacy Mag Rail skin not displaying on your arm

## Tweaks And Bug Fixes
* Added two new graffitis to Stack
* Added back Fragment's camera for when you're in the loading screen
* Fixed an infinite loading issue
* Fixed some fling bugs with fans
* Fixed snow footstep sounds
* Fixed cranes not being perfectly aligned at their regular position during the tutorial or trials/challenges
* Fixed time trial restart flash sometimes lasting longer than it should
* Added back Zephyr's interior
* Fixed some environmental errors
* Fixed text scaling badly on many UI if you increase text size in Roblox settings
* Snow is no longer transparent on slopes (doubles as an optimization!)
* Swapped gilded jewelry and regular jewelry valuable models
* Fixed a lighting issue affecting Downtown during the morning time
