Alpha 1.06 was released on December 14, 2023 (build 81).

## Changes

* Party members now appear on the compass (this is bugged right now, being worked on)
* Fixed some respawn antennas being impossible to reach
* Fixed gear being unclickable in the "PLAYER" tab
* Fixed tooltips lingering when they shouldn't
* Fixed player list arrows being visible when they shouldn't be
* Fixed party highlights
* Slightly buffed jump boosting
* Adjusted a couple time trial times to account for recent movement changes
* Fixed "slide boosting"
* Transitioning to a slide from a powerslide now resets powerslide's cooldown
* Fixed NAN'ing out of barrier (fences etc.) vaults, also fixed any other cases this may occur when starting a wall run/climb
