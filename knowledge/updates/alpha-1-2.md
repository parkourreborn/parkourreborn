Alpha 1.2 was released on May 31, 2024 (build 125).

## Changes

* **NPCs**
* At long last, Verna is now inhabited by non-player characters that you can talk to, trade with, and more! In future updates, the cast of NPCs will be ever-increasing, so stay tuned!
* Make sure you talk to Julian! He's right outside the tutorial!

  * **Valuables**
* Valuables are collectible items that can be sold to NPCs for credits. Currently, you can find them mainly around Downtown, however just like NPCs, their presence, variety, and depth will expand with future updates!

  * **Delivery Missions**
* You may occasionally be notified of a nearby package available for pickup.
* If you accept and collect the package, you must deliver it to the marked location without dying.
* Successfully completing a delivery will earn you credits.

  * **Premium Items**
* The store now contains its first premium upgrades available for purchase with tokens!
* Signal Booster: Respawn anywhere, infinitely
* Scanner Module: Detect collectible items in your vicinity, even through walls!

  * **Zipline Hook**
* Can be purchased from an NPC shop
* The player can no longer ride ziplines by default, they must equip the Zipline Hook
* The Zipline Hook's future upgrades will allow the players to create their own ziplines, and even ride UP ziplines!

  * **Runner Phone**
* Can be purchased from an NPC shop
* Allows the player to recieve text messages from NPCs, such as receiving a delivery location

  * **Runner Card**
* Acquired from Julian
* Can be shown to certain NPCs as proof of your rank/reputation

* Inventories have been reset, as the locations of some items have been moved/reconfigured

* AFK sit animation (/e sit)
* Improved task scheduling, fixing bugs such as the grappler cursor lagging behind a frame
* Character tweens to new rotation upon AutoRotate re-enabling, this smooths out some areas of gameplay
* Polished menu and HUD in various places (we plan on integrating the fancy buttons as seen in the premium menus to the rest of the UI as well)
* Rotated mission track outline so it doesn't overlap with stuff
* Time Trial beams now fade out instead of continuing upwards to infinity
* Mission tracker progress bar now displays a little flash when progress is made
* Added "world markers" functionality to track the position of objectives in the world
* Added "mission area" functionality that hides the marker when you're within a certain area around the mission objective
* Fixed a bug not allowing console players to rebind controls
* Fixed chat tags
* Fixed a few memory leaks
* Fixed entering a new district for the first time not giving you rank stars
* Several more bugfixes and changes we forgot about

We are aware of the issue with the loadout window!
The UI and functionality of the item scanner is temporary, and will be improved in an update in the near future!
