Alpha 1.3 was released on August 30, 2024 (build 146).

## Fragment
The Fragment district has been unlocked for exploration!

## Challenges
Challenges are a new type of race, as opposed to time trials.
* Challenges have checkpoints, more specific conditions for completion, and unique rewards
* Challenges do not have medals to earn, rather you are rewarded one time only upon completion of the challenge
* Currently available are several ability-unlock challenges to be discovered, as well as two advancement challenges

## Warp Core {{Icon|Robux}}
The Warp Core is a new premium upgrade that enables instant teleportation between any previously unlocked checkpoints. It can be purchased in the premium store for 300 Tokens.

## Dropkick
* The Dropkick is a new ability, unlockable through an ability book somewhere in Downtown.
* Dropkick requires you to be coiling to be used
* Press Attack while coiling to dropkick, this can hit players and knock them back based on your velocity

## Xp Routers
XP Routers when activated will permanently increase your XP (experience) multiplier.
* Routers are spread all throughout the map, and may add more or less to your multiplier based on their location
* Some things are not affected by your XP multiplier, such as mission/race rewards
* Parkour XP gain and combos however are affected by your XP multiplier

## Binoculars
Binoculars are a new Utility gear that allow you to zoom in on faraway sights and objects.
* Acquired after completion of a new questline from Derek

* New original track "Eden" by ball!
