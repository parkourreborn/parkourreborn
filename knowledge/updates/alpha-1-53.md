Alpha 1.53 was released on September 9, 2025 (build 189).

## Optimization
We have been hard at work optimizing the game! We hope you notice the increase in performance; we know that we do!
* Patched multiple memory leaks
* Optimized RenderFidelity of ~30,000 objects
* Optimized CollisionFidelity on ~30,000 objects
* Added DetailLevel to several thousand objects
* Disabled CastShadow on several thousand small objects
* Optimized several scripts
* Removed some unnecessary lights
* Optimized several widely-used textures

## Springhook Tweaks
Springhook needed some love! We only had the time to address one thing:
* Springhook should take away less speed when springboarding at lower vertical speeds

## Community Billboards
Thank you to our lovely community members for making an abundance of stunning billboard advertisements in our latest billboard contest! You’ll see them scattered around the map, adding to the immersion of a lively city!

## Miscellaneous Tweaks
* Made Content Creators their intended color (yellow) instead of the staff color (pink)
* In NPC shops, moved the “plus” and “minus” buttons on items to the right side of the shop to prevent people from accidentally purchasing the last item in the stack with Tokens (we’re sorry!)
* Made all in-world gear parts show up as purple on the premium item radar to reduce confusion
* Removed the toggle icon from the Paint Palette in the Premium Inventory
* Made it clearer who you were selecting when gifting Tokens
* Staff can now make in-game announcements both globally and server-side

## Bug Fixes
* Fixed some gear parts being unanchored
* Fixed a bug allowing the use of gear in challenges or trials
* Fixed being able to teleport with the map during trials in some cases
* Accessory VFX has been disabled since it often got in the way of first person
* Moved a pane of glass on Highrise back in place
* Fixed some exterior issues with the Golden/Ruby Vent buildings
* Fixed some misplaced valuable spawns
* Fixed some misplaced delivery endpoints
* Fixed some misplaced race endpoints
