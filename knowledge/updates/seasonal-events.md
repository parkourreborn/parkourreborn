## Halloween (2026)
Alpha 1.731 included the Halloween update. Similarly to the previous year, there are many map changes, decorations, badges, and easter eggs.

* **MAP CHANGES:** Halloween themed lighting. Some environmental props, such as checkpoints, have been reskinned for the event.
* **NPC OUTFITS:** NPCs with Halloween costumes will change into their seasonal outfits (excludes some NPCs such as Mantra and Marcel.
* **CANDY:** Floating candy can be collected around the map to earn XP. Collecting enough candy grants a seasonal Roblox badge.
* **EXPLODING PUMPKINS:** Explosive pumpkins spawn around the map, granting players instant speed when touched.
* **HIDDEN HARVEST EVENT (BADGE):** Jack o'Lantern's are spread around the map. Activating one lights it up, and finding all of them grants a the Hidden Harvest Roblox badge.

## Christmas (2025)
Alpha 1.7 included some Christmas related changes to the map and the game, including a limited Christmas cosmetic bag in the shop (players who log in during Christmas Eve/day will receive a free bag), new scenery around the Crystal time trial, and NPC outfit changes. Snow is automatically turned on for the month of December, and will turn off in March. Furthermore, there are many snow optimizations and improvements.

## Halloween (2025)
Alpha 1.6 included the Halloween update, including various seasonal decorations, badges, and easter eggs.
* **MAP CHANGES:** The overall ambiance and color of the map changed. Daytime and nighttime have an orange and purple hue respectively, window colors are orange, and bats fly around the map. There are also many decorative features, including pumpkins, fall foliage, Halloween props, and more
* **NPC Outfits:** Quest and vendor NPCs are dressed for the occasion.
* **CANDY:** Floating candy around the map gives XP. Collecting 25 gives the Halloween Roblox badge.
* **EXPLODING PUMPKINS:** Running into a pumpkin with an explosive attached to it will launch the player forwards. It can be used to maintain chains and momentum during a run.
* **HIDDEN HARVEST EVENT (BADGE):** Jack o'Lantern's are spread around the map. Activating one lights it up, and finding all of them grants a the Hidden Harvest Roblox badge.
