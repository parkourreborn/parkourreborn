Pre-Alpha 1.1 was released on November 5, 2023 (build 37).

## Changes

PSA: If you're on Mac and your first person is gray, turn your Viewmodel Opacity down to zero in the game settings!

  * **NEW TIME TRIALS**
* Riser, near the south east corner of Downtown
* Vestibule, near the base of the tallest crane

* Added bookmarks to the net
* Made Solar platinum medal slightly easier
* Improved first person camera rotation

* Improved asset preloading
* Big backend data structure refactor (this took a lot of time but it will save a ton more later)
