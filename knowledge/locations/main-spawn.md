* Type: Site (Building)
* Located: East from the Tutorial

## Overview

Main Spawn is a designated building/checkpoint in Downtown. As the name implies, it serves as the primary spawn point of the map. The Glass time trial can be found on the roof, and a red door that leads to a small interior underneath.

## Trivia

* Using the 'Return to Spawn' button at the bottom of the settings menu will take you here.

* The Main Spawn used to be an actual physical Roblox spawn plate. It was made up of neon-white material with hints of black that rotates on top of a building, but was removed for unknown reasons.

* The adjacent building's glass-cement walls are colored red in reference to Legacy's spawn sites.

* One of the Magnetic Coils can be found in the interior.
