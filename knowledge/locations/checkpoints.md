## Overview

Checkpoints serve as one of the primary spawning mechanics in the game. They are small metal objects with a blinking light on top of them distributed around the map. They can be synced to using ALT ACTION , which will make you respawn at them until you sync to another one, or enter a spawn region's vicinity.

Syncing to a checkpoint which you haven't synced to before will reward you with some XP. Undiscovered checkpoints blink green instead of blue, and are much brighter.

Using the warp core via the 'FAST TRAVEL' button in the tab menu, players can instantly teleport to them from across the map. The signal booster premium upgrade allows you to respawn anywhere without needing to sync to checkpoints.
