## Info
* Type: Site (Building)
* Located: West from PRK Tech

## Overview

Nimbus is a building with an exterior that follows a similar color scheme to the ones around it, consisting of gray concrete and blue wrapped all around. It houses the Leg Day challenge on the roof, which is necessary to unlock wall kicking. Its title is clearly displayed on the exterior, as well as some other buildings in the immediate vicinity. Nimbus also has a very spacious interior, connected to an open balcony on its bottom floor.

## Trivia

* Nimbus shares its name with an unreleased time trial.

* Nimbus was once thought to be in Fragment, but was later disproved by Iris.
