* Type: Site (Tower)
* Located: Southwest edge of Downtown
* Spawn Region(s): Yes

## Overview

Apollo is a tower primarily made up of a cement texture and a blue grid-like window pattern along the sides. It has several decorative features surrounding it, including billboards and vents. The interior is accessible through some doors on the front side of the building, or by breaking through the windows arranged around it. Pipes are distributed all around and lead up the building, allowing players to reach the top.

Apollo's interior has about 4 floors, all with various furniture such as chairs, tables, cubicles, and vending machines. The majority of the tower can be climbed through the inside as well.

## Trivia

* Apollo was reworked in alpha 1.5, similar to Vertigo and Artemis, receiving an interior.

* Apollo used to have a time trial named after it that would go through its interior.

* It was added in pre-alpha 1.2 and removed in alpha 1.1.

* In Greek Mythology, Apollo is Artemis' twin brother. It is where both of the towers got their names from.

* Additionally, it is the reasoning behind Phoebus' Graffiti being present within the tower, as they are both derived from the full name of "Phoebus Apollo."

* Just like Vertigo, Apollo has a blue screen TV with a dog on it.

* Apollo has one identically named checkpoint associated with it: Apollo.
