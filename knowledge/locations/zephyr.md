## Overview

Zephyr is Fragment's challenge tower located in the Highrise sector. It's the tallest tower in Fragment, and currently the tallest in the map. It's primarily composed of white cement and blue glass, along with a blue and green color scheme. Its name and logo can be found moving vertically on billboards placed on the exterior, as well as on the wall directly in front of the entrance. Zephyr's name is displayed in both English and Greek (ζεΦυρ) letters.

On the inside, Zephyr follows the same modern design and color scheme, with white concrete and light blue-tinted glass throughout. The interior is decorated with tables and chairs, plants, lighting, and waterfalls. Players must climb some parts of the exterior in order to reach the main entrance.

Just like Vertigo and Artemis, players must traverse a series of rooms to reach the top. Compared to the other towers, Zephyr takes the longest to climb. As players progress, a series of elevators will take them to different levels of the tower, which will eventually lead them to the outside roof area. From there, players can climb the remaining outside part of the tower to reach its respawn transceiver at the top.

## Trivia

* Prior to alpha 1.5, Zephyr's tower name was not known, and it was usually referred to as Highrise.

* It is now clear that Zephyr is the name of the tower, which is placed within Highrise.

* Zephyr, as well as Highrise, was initially planned for beta, but ended up releasing sooner in alpha 1.5.

* Currently, Zephyr takes the longest to climb compared to any other tower.
