## Info
* Type: Site (Tower)
* Located: West side of Fragment

## Overview

Legacy Foundation is a tower consisting of gray concrete, a light blue window/glass gridded pattern, and tarps wrapping around some parts of the building. It also has an interior with multiple floors. This is also the first location players will travel to as part of Derek's questline.

## Trivia

* Legacy Foundation is a callback to Reborn's predecessor, Legacy.

* The two vents on the roof are the same model used throughout Legacy.

* The avatar shown on a wall on the rooftop is Technically, a close friend of hudzell.

* Lunari is a developer on PARKOUR Legacy, and her graffiti can be found against a wall on the roof.

* Inside a section of the building on the roof is an easter egg referencing PARKOUR Legacy: there's an among us character shaped vent, along with other players (presumably known Legacy players). This building section is only accessible via clipping through it in some way.
