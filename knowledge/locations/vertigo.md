## Info
* Type: Site (Tower)
* Located: Northeast from the Tutorial
* Spawn Region(s): Yes

## Overview

Vertigo is the tallest tower in Downtown, subsequently making it one of the tallest in the map. It consists of white concrete, along with with a blue gridded window pattern. Its entrance is found on one of its support legs to the north, towards the bottom of the tower.

Vertigo's interior is a series of rooms which players must traverse to reach a lounge. From there, an elevator can be taken to a balcony near the top. The rest of the building is climbed from the outside.

## Trivia

* Vertigo shares its name with a tower in Legacy. Similarly, it is close to spawn, but is far shorter, and cannot be climbed from the interior.

* Vertigo was removed in pre-alpha 1.4 for remodeling purposes.

* It was added back in alpha 1.0, but in an unfinished state.

* Its rework was finished in alpha 1.1.

* The ambience dynamically changes upon entering/exiting the tower as of alpha 1.2.

* Prior to alpha 1.4, the platforms leading down to the hidden bar (in the ascent 2/3 area) didn't have any collision.

* Vertigo's exterior logo has been removed and changed a few times:

* The logo was a vertical oriented neon blue sign on the front of the building in pre-alpha 1.4 and prior.

* When it returned in alpha 1.0, it had no logo.

* In alpha 1.5, its new logo is presented on the front, back, and the inside of the entrance.

* In alpha 1.52, the balcony area received some updates.
