## Info
* Type: Site (Building)
* Located: Center of Downtown

## Overview

The Tutorial serves as an introduction to basic abilities and mechanics for new players. Players will spawn here when joining the game for the first time. Once finished, it rewards the player with 10 rank stars.

It teaches everything about the base moveset, including vault, mantle, springboard, wall climb, wall run, and more. It also teaches basic mechanics, such as momentum. On some sections, there are hologram animations that show how to perform the movement. The walkthrough can be skipped by using the elevator at the start, through a gray door on the right. It can be replayed by using the elevator inside of the Crossroads checkpoint building, accessible through a red door.

## Trivia

* Treetorial, a name dubbed by the community, refers to a tree situated on top of the building with grass at the end of the Tutorial. Specifically, it is the one closest to the player after landing on the grass.

* A TV can be found playing a short video showcasing how to do the double [ WALL RUN ] section of the Tutorial (view the gallery below).

* Upon finishing the Tutorial, the title card for Downtown displays itself as a sort of welcome message.

* It's a common misconception that this means the Tutorial building isn't part of Downtown, even though it is.

* Its successor, the Advanced Tutorial, will be an introduction to all unlockable abilities.

* Some base mechanics aren't explicitly taught by the Tutorial, such as high jump & coyote time.

* Since alpha 1.14, the ambience is adjusted while in the Tutorial building.

* One resource is located in the Tutorial building, that being one of the 'duct tape' resources.

* Prior to alpha 1.6, the tutorial began inside and the majority of the walkthrough was outside on a tall concrete structure. It's now redesigned to take place inside, and includes many new changes.

* It explains certain mechanics better (momentum, springboarding, wallrunning).

* Introduces hologram demonstrations.

* Better checkpoints and fail zones.

* The Genesis time trial was originally placed inside the start of the old Tutorial. It has now been brought outside at the bottom of the tall concrete structure.

* There used to be a YouTube walkthrough on the official A Mongoose Studio channel, but it was made private, presumably because it was outdated.
