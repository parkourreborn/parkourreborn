## Overview

Artemis is the tallest tower in Dirwik, subsequently making it one of the tallest in the map. It is composed of mostly cement, with some sections being made up of a blue gridded window-like pattern along the sides, akin to other towers such as Vertigo. Its name and logo are clearly shown along the sides of its exterior in bright neon blue. Its entrance is located near The Highpoint café, meaning players must climb the surrounding outside area before accessing the interior, and subsequently beginning their ascent of the challenge tower.

Just like Vertigo, Artemis features a series of rooms which players must traverse to reach the top. It contains many long vertical passageways, corridors, and rooms. The final floor is the CEO lounge, and the 'winch mount' component can be found on the round table. Climbing past this point will lead to the roof and reveal a respawn transceiver.

## Trivia

* The name "Artemis" was initially coined by the community, and has since been recognized as the official name.

* In Greek Mythology, Artemis is Apollo's twin sister. It is where both of the towers got their names from.

* Artemis is currently the second tallest accessible tower in the game.

* Artemis is the second tower to receive a rework after Vertigo, resulting in its climbable interior.

* Artemis is most likely a significant corporate hub in Dirwik, and is evident by the CEO lounge and security management ward.

* When sunlight hits the winged logo directly, it turns green, matching the same green gradient seen in the CEO lounge.

* Artemis is the first building in the game that utilizes a one-way fire exit door (located after descent 1).

* The other one is in Stack Hideout.

* The easter egg in the security management ward of Matti is a re-animated version of an actual YouTube video.
