## Info
* Type: Site (Building)
* Located: Northeast from Dirwik's crane

## Overview

PRK Tech (or PARKOUR Technology) is a large building comprised of multiple interior floors, and is identifiable by its name and logo on its east and west sides. Both instances of the logo are placed slightly differently, with one right below a long strip of lights, and the other affixed directly on the building's concrete.

The interior is decorated with furniture, plants, and elevators; one is operational, and can be used to travel between the second and third floor. The first and second floors contain rows of empty cubicles. Each floor has doors that lead to outside balconies. Additionally, you can find one of the 'nanotube cable' resource inside one of the cubicles on the second floor.

## Trivia

* PRK Tech is the second location players will travel to when completing Derek's questline.

* PRK Tech used to house two of the 'grappler mechanisms' resource. As of alpha 1.4, it only houses one of the 'nanotube cable' resource.
