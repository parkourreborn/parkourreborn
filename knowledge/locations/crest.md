## Info
* Type: Site (Tower)
* Located: Northern side of Fragment
* Spawn Region(s): Yes

## Overview

Crest is a large, beige colored tower covered in billboards, with many ziplines going to and from it.

## Trivia

* Just like Artemis, Crest was named by the community, as the tower looks similar to its identically named counterpart from Legacy.

* As of alpha 1.7, an identically named checkpoint has been added to the top of it, thus officializing the name.

* Perched on top of Crest's transceiver is a pigeon.

* Prior to alpha 1.31 when Crest received a proper ending interior, it was only fully scalable via advanced movement technique's.

* The 'EMAG accelerator' resource is featured in the boxes interior room (last gallery image below).

## Quick Facts

Crest is a large beige tower in Fragment covered in billboards and connected by many ziplines.

Important facts:
* Type: Site / Tower
* Location: Northern side of Fragment, below Highrise
* Spawn region: Yes
* District: Fragment
* Name started as community-created due to similarity to PARKOUR Legacy’s Crest
* Officialized when a checkpoint named Crest was added to the top in alpha 1.7
* A pigeon sits on top of Crest’s transceiver
