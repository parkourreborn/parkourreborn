## Overview

The Mag Rail is an augment gear situated on the left arm. When equipped, it allows the user to bounce off walls, slide up climbable props, and depending on the upgrade path, bounce toward drones.

There are enough resources to craft two Mag Rails. One of them can be upgraded toward the launch path (up to level 2).

The Mag Rail can be crafted with resources found at Harper's shop and a few scattered around the map:

* 1 Aluminum Casing (middle)

* 2 Magnetic Coils (sides)

* 3 Pulse Generators (sides)

* 1 Rail Trigger (sides)

It can also be bought from the store, with the base Mag Rail costing 400, Launch Rail I costing 530, and Launch Rail II costing 675.

## Upgrades

Each upgrade includes the benefits of the base and all previous tiers.

* BASE: Launches the user forwards in the direction they're looking while wall running, can slide up climbable props.

* LAUNCH RAIL: Increases launch strength, gives the ability to bounce upwards and redirect velocity along walls, more ammo, and the ability to pull yourself toward drones with magnetization.

* Level 1: +5% launch strength, can launch outwards at 45°, can bounce upwards and redirect near walls, & can magnetize toward drones.

* Level 2: +5% launch strength (10% total), can launch outwards at 90°, can bounce upwards and redirect near walls, +1 ammo, refresh 1 wall run on use, +70% magnetize range, +25% magnetize power.

* Level 3: TBD

* CHARGE RAIL: TBD

Information on unreleased upgrades are subject to change.

## Usage

The Mag Rail's main functionality stems from launching off walls and bouncing to redirect velocity. Both upgrade paths of this gear can be used on climbable props such as ladders and ropes. Most players will upgrade their Mag Rail to Launch Rail, furthering its capabilities. With Launch, players gain greater control over their movement, allowing them to launch at angles up to 90° at level 2.

Furthermore, they can redirect their velocity on walls with the bounce mechanic. A common technique involves launching from a wall run toward another wall with USE AUGMENT , bouncing with another USE AUGMENT , then pressing UP MOVEMENT to propel upward.

Additionally, the launch upgrade path gives you the ability to magnetize to drones, allowing you to bounce toward them when in range.

## Trivia

* The Mag Rail is a reworked version of Legacy's Mag Rail. They both share similarities, such as being able to bounce along the side of walls.

* Mag Rail was teased as a "secret gear" in one of hudzell's livestreams.

* It is one of the few gears to be introduced along with its model.
