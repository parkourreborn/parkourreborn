## Overview

The Quickbelt is a gear that will be added in a future update after alpha 1.5. This gear wraps around the waist and right leg, allowing the user to bind consumables to a hotkey for quick access. Without it, consumables can only be used while standing still on the ground. It will be able to hold a certain amount of consumables, and upgrading it will increase the amount that you can hold (there are no upgrade paths). The method of obtainment and specific upgrade information is currently unknown.

## Trivia

* The Quickbelt was initially planned for alpha 1.5, but was delayed.

* The Quickbelt will most likely be a utility gear.

* Its model was first teased on April 20, 2025.

* The Quickbelt is currently the only known gear with a single upgrade path.
