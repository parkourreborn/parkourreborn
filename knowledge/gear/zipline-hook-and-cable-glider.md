## Overview

The Zipline Hook and Cable Glider are gears that both allow you to ride ziplines, and is necessary to do so. The Zipline Hook is a small metallic hook that can be obtained by Derek for 2,500 or 100, and the Cable Glider is a mechanical version of it obtained by Petra for 15,000 or 200. After a certain threshold, you begin to rapidly gain momentum as you continue to use it. As of now, only the base version and first level of Cable Glider are obtainable.

## Upgrades

* ZIPLINE HOOK: Allows you to ride down ziplines.

* CABLE GLIDER: Motorized control over the direction you go on ziplines.

* Level 1: Can brake while riding ziplines & ride up them for 7.5 seconds, with an acceleration value of 35.

* Level 2: TBD.

* ZIPLINE KIT: Grants the ability to place down your own ziplines, allowing you to throw them for range.

* Level 1: TBD.

## Usage

Although the Zipline Hook isn't as game-changing as most other gear due to being a compound type, it's still very useful in traversing long distances. This comes at the cost of being situational, since not all ziplines are the same length or placed in the most convenient locations. The Cable Glider functions in the same way as the Zipline Hook, with the added function of being able to slow down, change directions, and move upwards for a short time.

## Trivia

* Prior to alpha 1.2, players were able to ride ziplines gearless.

* Upon the release of alpha 1.2, the zipline animation was changed to reflect the player using the hook instead of their bare hands.

* This is properly shown by its model being added in alpha 1.3.

* The model also has a hitbox, meaning you can stand on other people's hooks.

* The Cable Glider was added in alpha 1.5.

* Prior to alpha 1.52, no zipline gear could be bought from the store.
