## Info
* Type: Core
* Source: Crafting
* Base Recipe: Work Gloves [x1], Duct Tape [x1], Rugged Synthetics [x3]
* Store cost: Base: 120, Climber I: 175, Climber II: 210, Drifter I: 175, Drifter II: 210, The Glove is a core gear strapped onto the right arm. When equipped, it affects the user's wall climbing and wall running.

## Overview

There are enough resources to craft two Gloves. One can be upgraded towards the drifter path (up to level 2), and the other towards the climber path (up to level 2). Each Glove can only follow one upgrade path, which cannot be changed once crafted.

The base Glove can be crafted with resources found throughout the map and bought from vendors:

1 Work Gloves (middle)

1 Duct Tape (side)

3 Rugged Synthetics (sides)

It can also be bought from the store, with the base Glove costing 120, Climber I/Drifter I costing 175, and Climber II/Drifter II costing 210.

## Upgrades

Each upgrade includes the benefits of the base and all previous tiers.

* BASE: +25% (+50% in actuality) wall run duration & increased wall climb height.

* CLIMBER: Focused on verticality. Increased power both wall climbing and boosting, extra push at the peak of climbs, and an additional maximum wall climb ammo. The second wall climb functions identically to wall running, meaning you can only perform it at a different angle from the first one.

* Level 1 Upgrade: increased wall boost power & extra push at peak of climb.

* Level 2 Upgrade: +1 max wall climbs.

* DRIFTER: Focused on horizontality. Further increased duration for wall running, an additional maximum wall run ammo, uncapped fall distance, and vertical speeds get converted into horizontal.

* Level 1 Upgrade: +25% (+75% in actuality, 125% total) wall run duration, +1 max wall run, and uncapped downward wall run distance.

* Level 2 Upgrade: Vertical speed converts into horizontal speed when wall running.

## Usage

The Glove is a very versatile gear, essentially buffing your two main base movements—wall climb and wall run—allowing you to scale and traverse in areas that would otherwise be difficult or nearly impossible. This makes climbing most buildings and clearing gaps effortless with the boost in wall climb height and wall run duration.

## Trivia

* Before the Glove came out in alpha 1.1, two of the Rugged Synthetics resource were accidentally left in the game upon the release of alpha 1.08.

* The total cost of the Base Glove for all purchasable resources is 3,950 (or 350 + 60 for the Duct Tape and Rugged Synthetics respectively).

* The Glove was revealed to be an addition coming in alpha 1.1 from a screenshot teasing both alpha 1.1's and alpha 1.2's contents. This was posted by hudzell in the #unverified_lounge channel of the game's Discord server.

* Upon the release of alpha 1.2, many of the Glove's resources were accidentally left out, causing it to be partially deleted. A hotfix resolved this issue shortly after, with the addition of the 2 missing Rugged Synthetics being added to Derek's shop.

* Having the glove equipped makes your wall climb animation change, indicating more grip (similar to real life).

* At certain angles and low momentum, you will still do the gearless wall climb animation.

* The "Last Wind Reach" property of the Climber Glove's first upgrade was originally part of the base moveset.

* Although inspecting the Base Glove indicates that it increases wall run duration by +25%, it was discovered by the community that it is actually a +50% increase in duration.

* This also happens to the drifter variant, but with an increase of +75%, confirming that both the Base and Drifter Glove increase wall run duration by 1.5x and not via percentage as shown in-game.

* The Glove received a proper model in alpha 1.5.

* It follows a similar design concept to Legacy's Gloves, resembling a cuboid shape that fits best on blocky avatars.

* The Legacy skin can be applied to the Glove, resembling the same look as the first glove from PARKOUR Legacy.

* The Glove is the first gear where all of its upgrade levels for both paths are obtainable.

## Quick Facts

The Glove is a core gear on the right arm.

It affects wall climbing and wall running.

There are enough resources to craft two Gloves. One can go toward Drifter and one toward Climber, but each Glove can only follow one upgrade path and cannot be changed once crafted.

### Base Glove
Effect:
* +25% wall run duration, but actually +50% in practice
* increased wall climb height

### Climber Glove
Focus: verticality.

Benefits:
* increased wall climbing power,
* increased wall boosting power,
* extra push at peak of climbs,
* additional maximum wall climb ammo.

Level 1:
* increased wall boost power,
* extra push at peak of climb.

Level 2:
* +1 max wall climb.

The second wall climb functions like wallrunning in that it must be done at a different angle from the first one.

### Drifter Glove
Focus: horizontality.

Benefits:
* further increased wallrun duration,
* additional wallrun ammo,
* uncapped fall distance,
* vertical speed conversion into horizontal speed.

Level 1:
* +25% wallrun duration, actually +75% and 125% total,
* +1 max wallrun,
* uncapped downward wallrun distance.

Level 2:
* vertical speed converts into horizontal speed when wallrunning.
