## Overview

The Springhook is a core gear situated on the right arm. When equipped, it enhances ledge grabs by increasing both the distance and speed at which they can be initiated, while also allowing the user to launch from them. The Springhook and first level of Powered Springhook are both obtainable.

There are enough resources to craft two Springhooks. One can be upgraded towards the Powered Springhook path (up to level 1).

The base Springhook can be crafted with resources found at Connor's shop and a few scattered around the map:

* 1 Springhook Chassis

* 1 Frame Extension Unit

* 1 Hook Latch

* 2 Steel Extension Springs

It can also be bought from the store, with the Base Springhook costing 225 and Powered Springhook I costing 325

## Upgrades

Each upgrade includes the benefits of the base and all previous tiers.

* BASE: +50% ledge grab max fall speed, +60% ledge grab distance, hold to launch from ledge grabs.

* POWERED SPRINGHOOK: Greatly increases ledge grab max fall speed and grab distance, as well as converting vertical velocity into horizontal.

* Level 1: +10% ledge grab max fall speed (+60% total), +30% ledge grab distance (+90% total), +85% velocity transfer, hold to launch from ledge grabs.

* Level 2 (UNOBTAINABLE): +20% ledge grab max fall speed (+70% total), +30% ledge grab distance (+90% total), +95% velocity transfer, hold to launch significantly from ledge grabs.

Information on unreleased upgrades are subject to change.

## Usage

Springhook's primary purpose is to amplify ledge grabs. With this gear equipped, players can grab on to a ledge from farther away and while falling at higher speeds, depending on the upgrade level. Holding ASCEND allows the user to launch from the ledge. The Powered Springhook further improves this by increasing the ledge grab max fall speed and distance, while also transferring vertical velocity into horizontal. This makes it useful for recovering from falls and chaining ledge grabs.

## Trivia

* The Springhook was added in alpha 1.52.

* Compared to other gears, Springhook had the longest period between when it was teased and released.

* Many of its components can be found displayed in the Workshop, the interior area where Connor works.

* In alpha 1.54, Springhook received a change: if you use it without holding an input keybind, you will be launched upwards according to your vertical and horizontal velocity.
