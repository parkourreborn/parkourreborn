## Overview

The Binoculars are a small utility gear equipped on the hip that allows players to view distant objects. They can be obtained by completing Derek's questline, which requires a minimum rank of 5.

## Usage

Despite only being a utility type of gear, the Binoculars serve as a useful tool for spotting things from afar due to its high magnification. Furthermore, they only take up a non-important slot within the player's inventory, meaning other, more powerful gear like the Grappler wont be affected by it being equipped. Zooming with the Binoculars displays detail objects in the viewed area. On console, the DPad can be used to adjust the zoom.

## Trivia

* The Binoculars are the first ever gear in the utility category to be introduced.

* The Binoculars physical model appearing on players was added in alpha 1.5.

* The Binoculars received an animation in alpha 1.6.

* The Binoculars are the only gear that you can't equip skins to.
