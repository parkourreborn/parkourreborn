## Info
* Type: Key Item
* Source: Derek for 500

## Overview

The Runner Phone is a 'key item,' and currently the only one accessible. It can be equipped from the loadout, allowing you to communicate with NPCs in certain scenarios; used for Julian's Parcel deliveries and Derek's questline.

## Trivia

* It must be equipped to receive delivery missions; Parcels will not spawn without it.
