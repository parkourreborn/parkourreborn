## Overview

The Grappler is an augment gear that appears as a small device situated on the left arm. When equipped, it allows the user to pull themselves towards any object or wall it is fired at; it also allows the user to swing in a pendulum motion on ziplines and grapple/swing points, which are flying white drones.

There are enough resources to craft two Grapplers. One can be upgraded towards the yank path (up to level 2), and the other towards the swing path (up to level 2). Each Grappler can only follow one upgrade path, which cannot be changed once crafted. Each level 3 upgrade will release in beta.

The base version can be crafted with resources found throughout Downtown, Dirwik, Fragment, and bought from vendors:

1 Winch Mount (middle)

3 Fittings (sides)

1 Nanotube Cable (sides)

1 Micro Pulse Driver (sides)

1 Magnetized Alloy Hook (sides)

It can also be bought from the store, with the Base Grappler costing 400, Yank I/Swing I costing 530, and Yank II/Swing II costing 675.

## Upgrades

Each upgrade includes the benefits of the base and all previous tiers.

* BASE: Pulls the user towards any object it is fired at. Can swing on grapple points and ziplines throughout the map.

* YANK: Greatly increased pull strength, much faster use speed, and can reset ammo while being used.

* Level 1: +25% pull strength & +1 ammo.

* Level 2: +100% windup speed & +100% throw speed

* Level 3 (UNOBTAINABLE): +25% (total +50%) pull strength & can reset while in use

* SWING: Longer rope length, ammo resets whenever swinging on grapple points or ziplines, and allows the user to swing on any surface. Swing II can swing on players.

* Level 1: +35% rope length & swing points reset ammo.

* Level 2: Swing on any surface.

* Level 3 (UNOBTAINABLE): +15% (total +50%) rope length & +1 ammo (total of 2)

The above swing information reflects the changes in alpha 1.72.

* Level 2: +1 ammo & swing points reset ammo

* Level 3 (UNOBTAINABLE): swing on any surface

## Usage

The Grappler is a versatile gear that is useful in many situations, especially when combined with movement abilities. Its primary function is to pull you towards the object it is fired at, allowing you to cross wider gaps. When used at a higher velocity than it pulls you, it will redirect your velocity; for example, you can grapple while falling to redirect your downward velocity, then maintain it with a wall run, or similar, to move across long distances quickly.

There are two types of pulls that the Grappler can perform.

* Base Speed Pull: Refers to using the Grappler at a slower speed than it pulls you, causing it to pull you at its base speed.

* Velocity Redirection Pull: Refers to using the Grappler at a faster speed than it pulls you, causing it to redirect your velocity with its pull.

The number of times you can use the Grappler before needing to land is referred to as ammo, and the amount is determined by the upgrade and its level. The crosshair visually represents this by shortening every time you use it while airborne.

## Trivia

* The yank and swing Grappler are akin to the Grappler and Mag Rope from Legacy, respectively.

* Using the Grappler while falling doesn't damage you. This was changed from Legacy, where it did.

* Prior to alpha 1.731, grappling onto a pane of breakable glass would break it instantly and cancel out your pull.

* The total cost of the Base Grappler if all components are bought rather than found is 43,250 if the Fittings are bought by Derek, 45,500 if the Fittings are bought by Petra, or 560.

* On January 13th, 2024, Divided uploaded a YouTube video supposedly including instructions on how to obtain the Swing Grappler. This turned out to be just a troll, but resulted in quite possibly the biggest community gaslight in PARKOUR's history, and will forever be cemented as a hilarious moment captured on camera.

* During the pre-alpha stage of the game, the Grappler was manually given out to players by developers and staff. It was also randomly obtained by two players—likely by accident, or due to a bug. It was consequently taken away from everyone other than staff/developers upon pre-alpha 1.4's release.

* For the first few minutes of alpha 1.12, players were able to buy the Yank Grappler III for 800 at a test shop block, which was made to test out vendors in the development place, but accidentally left in the main experience place too.

* In alpha 1.13, another test shop block (presumably the same one as before) was accidentally left in a different spot far outside of the map, near the out-of-bounds test area. It was reachable by abusing a flight glitch that was discovered around the same time.

* Both were removed shortly after, but players who managed to buy the Yank Grappler III got to keep it. As hudzell stated: "if people go through all that trouble to get yank 3, congrats".

* All players who bought the Yank Grappler III had their spent tokens refunded once alpha 1.2 came out due to the inventory wipe that occurred.

* In alpha 1.2 and 1.4, a few resources required for the Grappler were changed to be sold by vendors instead.

* When alpha 1.4 released, one of the three Nanotube Cables was accidentally left out of the game. It was later added in alpha 1.41.

* Your gravity increases when you begin to swing, which is why this gear feels the way it does.

* When the Grappler physics got changed in alpha 1.1, it was because this gravity bug was fixed. It was then reverted in the following update.

* The Grappler received its visible model on players in alpha 1.5.

* Swing 2 received the ability to swing on other players in alpha 1.6, although this is not mentioned in the gear tooltip in the inventory.

* In alpha 1.72, the Swing upgrade path was modified so that players could swing anywhere at level 2 instead of 3. The rope length for each level was also modified.

* During the public testing phase of alpha 1.73, Swing 4 was handed out to players. It is a powerful variant of the Swing Grappler that allows players to swing on anything with infinite ammo. Pressing the USE AUGMENT keybind a second time pulls the user towards the attached object at high speeds. It is not publicly accessible.

* The Grappler can also be used while climbing ladders. Players let go of the ladder automatically when used.

## Quick Facts

The Grappler is an augment gear on the left arm.

It lets the player pull toward objects or walls it is fired at.

It also allows swinging in a pendulum motion on ziplines and grapple/swing points.

There are enough resources to craft two Grapplers. One can go Yank and one can go Swing. Each Grappler can only follow one upgrade path.

### Base Grappler
Effects:
* pulls the user toward any object it is fired at,
* can swing on grapple points and ziplines.

### Yank Grappler
Focus: stronger, faster pull.

Level 1:
* +25% pull strength,
* +1 ammo.

Level 2:
* +100% windup speed,
* +100% throw speed.

Level 3, unobtainable:
* +25% pull strength, +50% total,
* can reset while in use.

### Swing Grappler
Focus: longer swing and more swing freedom.

Level 1:
* +35% rope length,
* swing points reset ammo.

Level 2:
* swing on any surface.

Level 3, unobtainable:
* +15% rope length, +50% total,
* +1 ammo.

Swing II can swing on players.
